## What we learned
- Etsy: titles may have at most 3 words starting with two capitals and one "&". The lead photo must be re-ranked explicitly on existing listings.
- AI art: pro images are too expensive. The recipe is now cheap flash drafts plus one final, capped at $5/month, with pro blocked.
- Trend desk: 5 scans in a row found nothing approvable (mostly song lyrics or IP). Scans are now twice a day; the owner hasn't yet answered whether to cut them to once a day.
- QA catches real problems. Most items needed a second round; TpT 002 needed three.

## Traffic is the problem
One view in four days across 17 listings. New Etsy shops usually take 1–3 weeks to get impressions, but the 10 Oct turnaround report will decide on:
- leaning harder into the seasonal formats with the best search-to-listing ratios (Christmas bingo, coloring pages, cross-stitch)
- whether to keep spending on cut files, which have the most competition.
