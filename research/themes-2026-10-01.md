# Theme research — 2026-10-01 (Researcher)

## Summary
The queue was rebuilt from 10 unchecked themes (6 gothic) to 18 (3 gothic, 17%; never adjacent). Christmas demand for cut files peaks in Nov–Dec (e.g. "christmas svg" ~18k searches in Nov 2025 and ~22k in Dec, then under 500 in Jan), so the first 9 slots are winter/Christmas themes the Designer can finish by roughly Oct 6–10 at 2 bundles a day. That is well before the mid-November listing cutoff. The strongest non-gothic Christmas openings are snowflakes, stars/moons, reindeer/deer, nutcrackers, gingerbread and winter birds: each has high seasonal search volume against relatively few listings. Year-round nature subjects (bees, butterflies/dragonflies, fox/hedgehog/squirrel, hummingbirds, frogs, mushrooms) have flat, steady demand and moderate competition, and they suit the moody-natural Duskwood look. Two Valentine sets (one gothic, one botanical) are placed to be listed by early January, and a spring bunny/wildflower set is placed for the Easter peak (list by mid-Feb). Autumn and Thanksgiving were left out: "fall svg" peaked in Sep 2025 and "thanksgiving svg" peaks in early Nov, so a new listing now would not have time to rank. Three gothic themes kept their place (Raven & mistletoe, Haunted gingerbread house, Gothic Valentine). Gothic gift tags and Dark New Year's Eve were dropped. Spooky winter village was replaced with a non-spooky woodland village.

## Method and caveats
- Etsy.com (search pages, Seller Handbook) returns HTTP 403 to automated fetches, so I could not read live Etsy result counts or bestseller badges directly.
- Keyword volume and competition below come from **RankHero keyword pages** (third-party estimates of Etsy search volume; "competition" is their estimate of the number of competing Etsy listings). They show relative demand, not exact Etsy figures. URL pattern: `https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/<keyword>` (e.g. https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/snowflake-svg). Pages were fetched 2026-10-01 and cover Sep 2025–Aug 2026.
- Etsy's own named trends come from Etsy's 2025 holiday trend release, as reported by eRank and the press. Etsy has not yet published a holiday 2026 report that I could find.
- Blog-sourced claims are labelled **(blog)**.

## Ranked queue with reasoning
1. **Botanical frost snowflakes.** This is the best-evidenced Christmas term (≈22k Dec searches vs ≈20k listings). The design is snowflake crystals built from pine, fern and berry sprigs, so it reads as nature, not gothic, and stays visually distinct from 001. Each snowflake is naturally one connected shape. It sells until January.
2. **Celestial Christmas.** Stars and moons have high volume with low difficulty, and Gothmas named celestial motifs. Moons and stars are very readable silhouettes and core to Duskwood.
3. **Raven & mistletoe (gothic 1/3).** Mistletoe has very thin competition, and raven is a steady year-round term. It is the most nature-led of the gothic options.
4. **Woodland reindeer & stag ornaments.** Reindeer and deer volumes are strong in Nov–Dec. Antlers make clear, connected silhouettes.
5. **Nutcracker ballet silhouettes.** About 1.6k Nov searches on only ~3k listings, plus Etsy's Nutcrackercore trend. Nutcracker figures (holding no sabre or other weapon), ballerinas, mice in tutus and sugar-plum shapes. The Nutcracker story is public domain, so avoid any film or stage-production branding.
6. **Haunted gingerbread house (gothic 2/3).** Gingerbread is a solid Christmas term and fits the Gothmas angle. Kept but demoted from #2.
7. **Winter songbirds (cardinal, chickadee, owl).** Owl is year-round with a Christmas bump, and cardinal has low competition. Bird silhouettes are clean.
8. **Mushroom & toadstool winter woodland.** Mushroom demand is steady with moderate competition. Starts the year-round mushroom line.
9. **Snowy woodland village silhouettes.** Replaces "Spooky winter village". A winter-village laser file is among the top weekly SVG sellers (EtsyHunt). Skyline silhouettes cut as one shape.
10. **Bees & honeycomb botanicals.** Bee is a flat ~1.6k/mo, year-round term with difficulty 36.
11. **Gothic Valentine (gothic 3/3).** Goth Valentine cut files appear among the top SVG sellers. List by early January.
12. **Butterflies & dragonflies.** Butterfly volume is high, and dragonfly is a low-competition gap. Year-round.
13. **Botanical Valentine (floral hearts, lovebirds, doves).** Heart svg is a huge base term, and dove has low competition. This gives a non-gothic Valentine option. List by early January.
14. **Woodland animals (fox, hedgehog, squirrel, rabbit).** Year-round with low competition on the specific animals.
15. **Birdwatching silhouettes.** Year-round. Hummingbird is a low-competition gap.
16. **Spring garden bunnies & wildflowers.** Easter/spring peak in March. List by mid-February.
17. **Frogs & lily pads cottagecore.** Year-round whimsy with modest competition.
18. **Foraging mushrooms set 1.** Year-round. Kept late because #8 already covers mushrooms.

Gothic/spooky/dark: 3 of 18 (17%), at positions 3, 6 and 11, never adjacent. No neighbouring themes share a subject: the two mushroom sets, two Valentine sets, two bird sets and two building sets are each separated.

## Dropped themes and why
- **Gothic Christmas gift tags (printable PDF + SVG).** Part of it isn't a cut file. Tags depend on text, which reads poorly at thumbnail size. Demand is modest ("gift tags svg" ~720 Nov / ~1.6k Dec on ~18k listings). It would also be a 6th gothic-Christmas product after 001–005.
- **Spooky winter village silhouettes.** The village subject is strong, but the spooky angle adds nothing, and the gothic quota is better spent elsewhere. Replaced by the non-spooky Snowy woodland village (#9).
- **Dark New Year's Eve (moon-phase clock, champagne bats).** The selling window is one month ("new years svg" spikes only in December). Typical NYE designs are text-heavy, the champagne/alcohol motif adds little, and it would be a fourth gothic item.
- **Considered, not added:** Autumn/Thanksgiving (peak already passed; Thanksgiving svg peaks early Nov). Snowman, Santa and Christmas tree (large volumes, but crowded and far from the Duskwood look). Nativity (religious niche, off-look). Witch and tarot (spooky/occult quota already used). Generic "cottagecore/woodland/celestial svg" keywords (buyers rarely search these words; use them as secondary tags only).

## Rules check
All themes are original nature, seasonal or folklore subjects. No characters, brands, franchises or trademarked phrases, and no "Creepmas". Nutcracker designs must not include sabres or swords. Gothic Valentine anatomical hearts are decorative outlines only, with no medical claims.
