# TpT music research — 4 Oct 2026

Researcher, first TpT pass for the owner's store (brian-lotze). About 13 web searches and fetches.
**All figures are estimates.** Prices and review counts were read from TpT "Most Popular" browse pages on 4 Oct 2026 through a page-summarising fetch tool, so they may be slightly off or out of date. TpT doesn't publish sales numbers, so review count is the stand-in for sales. A common rule of thumb is that roughly 1 in 10–20 buyers leaves a review; that ratio is unverified. We take **subject and format only** from these. Never another seller's design, title wording or layout.

## 3. Saturated vs underserved (for what we can build in code)

Our formats: notation/rhythm worksheets, flashcards, bingo with unique cards, colour-by-note, posters, non-editable PDFs. Not yet: audio/video, Boom Cards, Google Slides.

**Saturated** (many sellers, but proven). Enter only with a clear angle:
- Holiday colour-by-note / colour-by-code for grades 1-5: at least 6 competing sets per holiday (S2–S5), plus free ones off TpT (S11). Our angle: tiered levels in one file (treble, bass, rhythm values) and an answer key that QA has checked.
- Christmas music bingo: two items with 280–440 reviews. Our angle: winter/non-denominational theme, plus truly unique cards generated in code (30 cards) and rhythm-pattern calling cards.
- Generic music word searches ($3, 50-puzzle packs). Low price, low value.

**Underserved or a good fit:**
- **HS / music-technology printables**: no DAW item in the HS top 20 (S6), and production units have ≤13 reviews (S8). Posters and the "elements of music" anchor-chart format earn up to ~956 reviews, but nobody offers it for music production terms. This fits the owner's line.
- **Middle-school no-tech sub plans**: few items (S7 shows a handful with 9–126 reviews) against strong K-5 demand. A music-tech or careers topic fits the owner.
- **K-2 rhythm flashcards / tracing with a fall or winter theme**: the tracing format has ~445 reviews but a non-seasonal cover. A seasonal K-2 rhythm set is a natural add-on.
- **Bingo with rhythm patterns** (not just symbols) for grades 3-5: the unique-card generator is our edge.

Avoid: anything based on copyrighted songs (Monster Mash, film/TV themes, carols in copyrighted arrangements, "lyric posters"). Public-domain folk songs and composers (Saint-Saëns' Danse Macabre, Tchaikovsky's Nutcracker) are fine as subjects.

## 4. Price points, bundles, freebies

- **Single seasonal printables: $3.00–$5.50** (most common $3.50–$5.25) (S2–S5). Mega packs and multi-activity sets: $10–$12.50. Bingo games: $4.99–$6.75. Sub plans: $3.50–$6 single, $9–$15 bundle or sub tub. Posters/bulletin boards: $5–$8. Production units: $7.50–$45, with big curricula at $70–$120 (S8).
- **Bundles**: sellers commonly discount 20–35% (e.g. $24 to $18; $18.72 to $12.50; $24.50 to $15; S3–S5). Seller advice (S9): list each part separately and also as a bundle. Aim for a price mix of about 10% under $3, 55% at $3–10 and 35% over $10. Building a series (Halloween, then Thanksgiving, then winter of the same format) makes a bundle possible by December.
- **Freebies** (S9): widely recommended for new stores because they drive follows, reviews and newsletter/search visibility. The owner's free Loop Project is already a freebie. Suggestion (estimate, not tested): make one free sample page per new paid line (e.g. 1 colour-by-note page or 1 poster) that links to the paid item. Don't make a whole seasonal set free.
- Store benchmark (S9, advice only): stores do better with many listings (often quoted as 50+), but quality matters more than count. Basic seller nets 55% minus $0.30 per sale (FACTORY.md), so a $5 item nets about $2.45.

## 5. Owner's store look (for the Designer / tpt/STYLE.md)

From the 4 cover JPGs on the store (S10, 350×350 thumbnails):
- **Palette**: monochrome. Black line art and black text on white, or on a **white-to-mid-grey vertical gradient** (Loop Project covers). No accent colour on the covers.
- **Brand mark**: a line-drawn **studio monitor speaker**, black outline with rounded corners and white fill, centred in the upper ~60% of the square. The small tweeter has curved text reading "DIGITAL MUSIC PRODUCTION". The large woofer holds a **Spartan/Trojan helmet wearing headphones**, in white on a black circle. This looks like the owner's own or school mascot mark. Treat it as the owner's logo: reuse it only if the owner provides the file, and don't redraw it as our own.
- **Type**: tall **condensed bold sans-serif in all caps** (close to Bebas Neue / Oswald) for titles, stacked on 1–2 centred lines below the speaker. Subtitle in a **light condensed sans, sentence case** (e.g. "Presentation").
- **Layout**: square, centred, logo on top and title block below, generous white space, no borders, no product photos.
- Exception: the Keyboard Shortcuts cover is landscape (350×233). It shows the product itself, a dark charcoal page with coloured key-label chips (purple, green, red, orange), a piano diagram and a chart, with a large diagonal grey "PREVIEW" watermark.
- Implication for the new K-8 line: the black/white condensed-caps style suits HS items as it is. For K-5 classroom items, the Designer may need a lighter variant: same condensed caps title band and centred layout, plus colour from the activity art. The Designer should decide this in tpt/STYLE.md, and the owner may want to confirm.
