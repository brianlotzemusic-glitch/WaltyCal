# Daily research refresh: 2026-10-05 (Researcher)

## Summary
The EtsyHunt bestseller lists still show "Updated October 1, 2026". The new evidence is RankHero keyword data for queued Christmas themes and eRank's September 2026 trend report, published 4 Oct. Christmas demand on Etsy peaks in November, and the coloring pilot, highland cow and nutcracker terms all peak then. So the Christmas items with the best search-to-listing ratios or proven sellers move up, ahead of year-round items, which can wait. Celestial Christmas moves down because its search demand is very low. 5 items moved and none were added (23 unchecked Etsy items remain, more than the 8 minimum). I made 16 searches and fetches. Evidence strength: **moderate for search demand, weak to moderate for sales**. All sales figures are single-source EtsyHunt estimates. Etsy.com Bestseller and "in X baskets" signals were not reachable (Etsy returned 403 on every earlier attempt), so I didn't try them again.
