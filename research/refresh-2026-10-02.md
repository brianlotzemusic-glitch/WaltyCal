**Rationale**
- **Christmas first among proven subjects.** Christmas buying started earlier this year (eRank: "christmas" in the top 20 a month earlier than in 2025), and a Christmas listing gains more from extra days of ranking time than a year-round one. Nativity (est. ~42/wk cross DXF + nativity in the ~37/wk laser mega bundle, EtsyHunt) and Highland cow Christmas (est. ~29/wk PNG, EtsyHunt; plus ~1.3k/mo "highland cow svg" search, RankHero est.) move ahead of year-round items. Botanical frost snowflakes moves up 2 because Witchy crystals moved down.
- **Witchy crystals demoted (not dropped).** The proven listing (est. ~60/wk, 93 lifetime, EtsyHunt) sells on its slogan text, which we drop. Without it, the subject's search demand is weak: "crystal svg" ~170/mo against ~32.9k listings (RankHero est.). It is year-round, so there is no seasonal cost to building it a few days later. It keeps the first gothic slot.
- **Rules check.** Gothic items are at positions 7, 10, 13 and 16 of 25 unchecked (4/25 = 16%), never adjacent. No near-duplicate neighbours (bird, Valentine and mushroom sets stay separated). [format] pilots sit at 2, 5 and 11. [pod] stays first. The header and all [x] lines are unchanged.
- **Timing.** The [pod] item is skipped, so there are 16 buildable items up to and including Winter songbirds, the last Christmas-relevant theme. At ~2 bundles a day the last Christmas theme is built around Oct 10–11, well before the ~Nov 15 cutoff. The 2-listings-a-day cap keeps listing in step.
