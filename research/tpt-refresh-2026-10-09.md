- Secondary: no January ensemble packs found, so a goals and practice-tracker item is a gap.

Order: Thanksgiving first, then the concert kit, winter note names, sub plans, solfege, bell-ringers, New Year, then evergreen items. The two older HS/MS items keep their 1 Nov and 8 Nov dates.
