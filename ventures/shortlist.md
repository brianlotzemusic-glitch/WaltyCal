# Venture shortlist: combined ranking (Scout, 2026-10-09)

This ranking combines two research runs:
- `research/2026-10-01.md` (A): design and digital-file marketplaces.
- `research/2026-10-01-b.md` (B): websites, software, other marketplaces, content, data and affiliate programs.

Both runs are scored on the same weights:
- profit at 6 months 15% and at 12 months 20%
- startup cost 10%
- owner hours 15%
- automation fit 15%
- policy risk 10%
- synergy 10%
- time to first revenue 5%

Scores are weighted totals out of 5. Ties are broken by 12-month profit, then policy risk. Revenue confidence is low because earnings data are blog-sourced or assumed.

1. **Laser-cut file shop (Etsy sister shop).** **Launcher's next pick (locked until Etsy gate is met).**
   - **Why:** reuses the factory's vector/DXF pipeline and Etsy API, sells to laser owners at higher prices than Cricut files, and can be fully automated.
   - **Startup:** about $40–55. **Owner setup:** about 1.5–2 h. **Ongoing:** about 15 min a week.
   - **Score 3.75.** Source: A.
2. **Atomm creator account (exclusive laser projects + parametric generators).**
   - **Why:** xTool's $1M program went live in Sept 2026. It pays 80% on premium projects, $0.10–0.23 per use on exclusive projects and $0.15 per generator download. AI work is allowed if disclosed, there is no application, and it is off Etsy. It is new, so its terms are unproven.
   - **Startup:** $0. **Owner setup:** about 45–60 min. **Ongoing:** about 10 min a week.
   - **Score 3.45.** Source: B.
3. **Gothic POD merch via Printify on Etsy.**
   - **Why:** the same designs become stickers, decals and mugs, and listing is automated through the Printify API.
   - **Startup:** about $40–60. **Owner setup:** about 1 h. **Ongoing:** 1–2 h a week on buyer messages about physical orders.
   - **Score 3.35.** Source: A.
4. **Free maker web-tool site** (layered-ornament, box and hinge generators) with affiliate links and a shop funnel.
   - **Why:** useful tools are what Google's policies protect, and AI summaries cannot replace them. It is pure code (deploy with git push) and owned traffic, but slow to rank.
   - **Startup:** about $12. **Owner setup:** about 1 h. **Ongoing:** about 0.
   - **Score 3.30.** Source: B.
5. **Own storefront (Payhip) + Pinterest.**
   - **Why:** a near-zero-fee mirror that hedges Etsy risk and builds an owned email list. Traffic is the whole problem.
   - **Startup:** $0–30. **Owner setup:** about 1–1.5 h. **Ongoing:** about 5 min per product upload.
   - **Score 3.20.** Source: A.
6. **Adobe Stock vectors.**
   - **Why:** accepts labelled generative-AI vectors and reuses the whole catalogue, but pays only about $0.25–0.33 per download.
   - **Startup:** $0. **Owner setup:** about 45 min. **Ongoing:** about 15 min a week. These owner times are estimates.
   - **Score 3.20.** Source: A.
7. **KDP gothic/holiday coloring books.**
   - **Why:** $0 to start with a decent 12-month ceiling, but the niche is saturated, the AI image-book crackdown is active, and there is no upload API.
   - **Startup:** $0–30. **Owner setup:** about 1 h. **Ongoing:** about 15 min per title.
   - **Score 3.15.** Source: A.
8. **Printable craft guides/handbooks** (laser ornament design handbook, gothic Cricut project book), sold on Payhip and Etsy.
   - **Why:** higher-value PDFs plus templates built from the factory's own design rules. It has no income claims and needs the Payhip traffic.
   - **Startup:** $0. **Owner setup:** 0 h beyond Payhip. **Ongoing:** about 5 min per product.
   - **Score 3.15.** Source: B.
9. **Silhouette Design Store / SoFontsy.**
   - **Why:** curated SVG marketplaces that accept AI-labelled work, but entry is by application and terms are undisclosed.
   - **Startup:** $0. **Owner setup:** about 1 h for the application. **Ongoing:** about 10 min a week.
   - **Score 3.05.** Source: A.
10. **Craft affiliate layer** (xTool 6–8%, Cricut 4–12%, Etsy via Awin 4%, Amazon about 3%) on the tool site, Payhip, newsletter and Pinterest. Never in Etsy listings.
    - **Why:** adds income on top of other ventures, but has no traffic of its own.
    - **Startup:** about $5 (refundable Awin deposit). **Owner setup:** about 1 h. **Ongoing:** about 0.
    - **Score 3.05.** Source: B.

**Just outside the top 10:**

- Creative Market: 3.00, A.
- Kit newsletter: 2.95, B.
- Gothic-craft content site: 2.90, B.

**Excluded or not recommended:**
- Envato (bans AI).
- Medium paywall (bans AI writing).
- Faceless AI YouTube and TikTok (monetization policies).
- Programmatic SEO (scaled content abuse).
- Spotify AI music (spam filter).
- Figma paid files (closed to new sellers).
- Glowforge Catalog (needs owning a Glowforge).
- Mobile apps (cost, and Google's 12-tester rule).
- Scraped datasets.
