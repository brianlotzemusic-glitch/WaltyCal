# Venture shortlist (Scout, 2026-10-01)

Full research: `research/2026-10-01.md`. Scores are weighted totals out of 5. Revenue confidence is low because earnings data are blog-sourced or assumed.

1. **Laser-cut file shop (Etsy sister shop).** **Launcher's next pick (locked until Etsy gate is met).** Reuses the factory's vector/DXF pipeline and Etsy API, sells to laser owners at higher prices than Cricut files, and can be fully automated. Startup about $40–55. Owner setup about 1.5–2 h. **Score 3.75.**
2. **Gothic POD merch via Printify on Etsy.** Same designs as stickers, decals and mugs, with listing automated through the Printify API. Owner must handle buyer messages about physical orders. Startup about $40–60 (samples). Owner setup about 1 h, then 1–2 h a week. **Score 3.35.**
3. **Own storefront (Payhip) + Pinterest.** Near-zero-fee mirror of the catalogue that hedges Etsy risk and builds an owned email list, but sales depend on building traffic. Startup $0–30. Owner setup about 1–1.5 h. **Score 3.20.**
4. **KDP gothic/holiday coloring books.** $0 to start with the best 12-month ceiling outside Etsy, but the niche is saturated, the AI image-book crackdown is active, and there is no upload API. Startup $0–30 (proof copies). Owner setup about 1 h, then about 15 min per title. **Score 3.15.**
5. **Silhouette Design Store / SoFontsy.** Curated SVG marketplaces that accept AI-labelled work, but entry is by application and terms are undisclosed. Startup $0. Owner setup about 1 h for the application. **Score 3.15.**
