# Scout report B: wider search beyond design marketplaces, 2026-10-01

**Scope:** report A (`research/2026-10-01.md`) covered design and digital-file marketplaces: laser files, Printify POD, Payhip + Pinterest, KDP, Silhouette, Teachers Pay Teachers, Redbubble, Shutterstock, Canva, Creative Fabrica, Design Bundles and Cricut. This run looks wider: owned websites, software, other template and asset marketplaces, content and audience businesses, data and knowledge products, and affiliate programs that fit the gothic/crafting audience.

**Factory assets assumed:** code-generated vectors (Python geometry to SVG/PNG/DXF), an Etsy API toolchain, 5 finished gothic holiday SVG bundles, and hourly AI workers that can write code, push to GitHub and call APIs with keys the owner provides. The owner has no laser cutter, 3D printer or Cricut, and does not want to appear on camera.

**Hard filters:**
- Startup cost of $100 or less.
- No inventory the owner stores, packs or ships.
- No licence, permits or regulated claims.
- AI-producible, and allowed under the platform's 2025–26 rules.
- No deception, fake engagement, spam, scraping against terms, or reselling other people's content.

**Confidence note:** platform rules, fees and thresholds come from primary pages wherever I could reach them (Google, YouTube, Notion, Figma, Shopify, Envato, Unity, itch.io, Atomm/xTool, Medium, Raptive and Mediavine help pages). Where only blogs were available I say so. Every revenue range in this report is my own assumption-driven estimate, not observed data. **Confidence in revenue figures: low.**

## 2. Every candidate considered

### A. Owned websites

1. **Free maker web-tool site.**
   - **Tools:** layered-ornament generator, finger-joint box and kerf generator, gothic monogram and name-tag generator, SVG layer splitter, living-hinge generator. All run in the browser, are hosted on Cloudflare or GitHub Pages, and are built from the factory's existing geometry code.
   - **Revenue:** ads once eligible, affiliate links, and a funnel to the shop and Payhip.
   - **Status:** **Kept.** These are useful, interactive pages, not mass content, so they fall outside the scaled-content definition. AI Overviews cannot replace a tool.
   - **Competition:** existing free tools such as boxes.py and MakerCase. Our angle is decorative and gothic generators.

2. **Gothic-craft tutorial and content site** (ads and affiliate). **Kept, but scored low.** Allowed only if each page is genuinely useful. Traffic is slow and shrinking because of AI Overviews, and Raptive's human-involvement rule makes a mostly-AI site unlikely to pass. The tool site (#1) does the same job with better defensibility.

3. **Programmatic SEO site** (thousands of template pages such as "free [X] SVG", "[holiday] [object] cut file"). **Excluded.** This is the exact pattern named in Google's scaled content abuse policy and the target of the Aug 2026 update.

4. **Directory site** (for example makerspaces, laser material suppliers, or free SVG sites). **Excluded or low.** The useful data has to be scraped or copied from other sites, which conflicts with the "no scraping, no reselling others' content" constraint. A hand-curated directory is too small to earn.

5. **Expired-domain or parasite-SEO plays.** **Excluded.** Google's "expired domain abuse" and "site reputation abuse" policies cover them, and they depend on deception.

### B. Software

6. **Atomm generators.** xTool's maker platform lets developers publish parametric design generators through a Developer Console and SDK. **Kept** and folded into candidate 16, because it is one account and one platform.

7. **Own micro-SaaS** (for example an SVG-to-layered-laser converter with a subscription). **Kept, but scored low.** The owner must open Stripe, handle refunds and support, and carry the tax and terms paperwork. The same tool earns more simply as a free page on the tool site (#1) or as an Atomm generator, with no billing.

8. **Chrome extension** (freemium via ExtensionPay). **Kept, but scored low.** Its only crafting use would be Etsy or Design Space helpers, and anything that reads Etsy pages risks Etsy's terms. Support and payments fall on the owner.

9. **Figma paid plugin.** **Kept, but scored low.** Figma approves new sellers for plugins (15% flat fee) but **not for paid files**. A cut-file prep plugin has a tiny audience and little synergy. ([Figma help](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/12067637274519-About-selling-Community-resources))

10. **WordPress.org freemium plugin.** **Kept, but scored low.** Trialware is banned and freemium is allowed. GPL code, heavy support load, little synergy. ([make.wordpress.org](https://make.wordpress.org/plugins/2018/08/23/guideline-update-clarifications-to-trialware-and-human-readability/))

11. **Shopify app.** **Kept, but scored low.** Developers keep 100% of the first $1M after a $19 registration ([Shopify dev](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/revenue-share)). However, merchant support and app review are heavy, and there is no synergy.

12. **API on RapidAPI.** **Kept, but scored low.** Nokia acquired Rapid in Nov 2024 and the public marketplace still runs ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/13/nokia-acquires-rapid-the-api-company-once-valued-at-1b/)). There is no demand evidence for anything we could build, and current fee terms were not found.

13. **Mobile apps** (iOS/Android). **Excluded:** see the table above (the $99 Apple fee, Google's 12-tester rule needs real people, and template-app rules).

### C. Template and asset marketplaces (not covered in report A)

14. **Notion templates** (gothic reading or "witchy" planners, a crafter's order and inventory tracker).
    - **Kept.**
    - **Fees:** Notion Marketplace takes 8% + $0.40 per sale; payouts need a $20 balance and funds are held 14 days.
    - **Rules:** every template is reviewed, and spam ("multiple versions, repetitive submissions") is prohibited. There is no explicit AI rule.
    - **Fit:** the market is saturated (blog-sourced) and synergy with vectors is low.
    - **Sources:** [Notion selling](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace), [Notion guidelines](https://www.notion.com/help/template-gallery-guidelines-and-terms).

15. **Framer and Webflow templates.**
    - **Kept, but scored low.**
    - **Framer:** reviews every template (1–3 weeks) against a design-quality bar. Blogs disagree on its commission.
    - **Webflow:** requires "Good" on every rubric area.
    - **Fit:** both are built visually in an editor, so automation fit is poor.
    - **Sources:** [Framer creator program](https://www.framer.com/help/articles/how-the-creator-program-works/), [Webflow help](https://help.webflow.com/topic/selling-templates), [allaboutframer (blog)](https://allaboutframer.com/where-to-submit-your-framer-templates-(and-actually-get-sales)).

16. **Atomm (xTool's maker community): exclusive laser projects plus generators.** **Kept. Top new find.**
    - **Program:** the $1M Creator Program went live about 21–22 Sept 2026.
    - **Earnings:**
      - Premium projects pay **80%** of sales.
      - Exclusive projects pay **0.1–0.23 Coins per valid use**, where 1 Coin = $1, 1 download = 1 valid use and 1 machine run = 2.
      - Generators pay **0.15 Coins per download**.
      - Material commissions are up to 30%.
      - Payouts are weekly, with a $20 minimum withdrawal.
    - **Bonus until 31 Dec:** 100 Coins for an exclusive high-quality project, or a generator, that reaches 300 uses or downloads within 90 days.
    - **Access:** project creators and generator developers do not need to apply.
    - **AI policy:** AI-generated content (AIGC) is allowed if disclosed on upload. Undisclosed AIGC is removed.
    - **Scale (company-stated):** 320K monthly active users, 16K creators, about 100K projects and 2M valid uses. That works out to roughly 20 uses per project on average, heavily skewed.
    - **Sources:** [Atomm creator program](https://www.atomm.com/blog/2822-creator-program), [Atomm AIGC policy](https://www.atomm.com/blog/1102-atomm-aigc-policy), [Atomm generator guide](https://www.atomm.com/blog/2043-how-to-use-generators), [PR Newswire 22 Sep 2026](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20260922cn53581/xtool-unveils-major-atomm-upgrade-introducing-the-worlds-first-ai-creation-suite-for-craft-makers), [what is Atomm](https://www.atomm.com/blog/2620-what-is-atomm).
    - **Caveats:**
      - The program says "no xTool machine required" only for generators and videos. Projects normally show photos of the finished make, which we cannot shoot, so projects may underperform or need rendered mockups.
      - Exclusive files cannot also be sold on Etsy. They must be separate designs.
      - Non-exclusive projects earn Credits, which appear to be a reward currency rather than cash.
      - The program is weeks old, so its terms may change.

17. **Glowforge Catalog designer.** **Excluded.** It requires a Glowforge Premium membership, so in practice the owner must own a Glowforge (cost far above $100). Earnings are an opaque formula. ([Glowforge FAQ](https://support.glowforge.com/hc/en-us/articles/1260804788069-Glowforge-Catalog-Designer-FAQ))

18. **Gothic dingbat and display fonts.** The factory's bats, moths, skulls and webs become an installable OTF/TTF dingbat font via fontTools.
    - **Kept.**
    - **Where to sell:** Payhip, Creative Market (AI label applies), itch.io (AI disclosure), and the existing Etsy shop.
    - **Rules:** MyFonts has no published AI rule and requires review for technical quality ([Monotype foundry support](https://foundrysupport.monotype.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028868051-Common-Reasons-for-Font-Rejection)).
    - **Fit:** demand is unverified, but dingbat fonts are a known Cricut and Design Space workflow (my inference, not sourced).

19. **itch.io game asset packs** (gothic icon sets, UI frames).
    - **Kept, but scored low.**
    - **Rules:** AI disclosure is mandatory, and untagged AI assets are delisted or de-indexed.
    - **Earnings:** forum anecdotes say €30–50 a year per asset and "~$50 from 20 sales".
    - **Sources:** [itch quality guidelines](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines), [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/software/platforms/indie-distribution-platform-itch-io-now-requires-asset-creators-to-disclose-the-use-of-generative-ai-in-their-work/), [itch forum](https://itch.io/t/1425337/advice-on-selling-assets-pixel-art).

20. **Unity Asset Store.**
    - **Kept, but scored low.**
    - **Terms:** a 70/30 split. AI content must be disclosed in an "AI description" field, and submissions may be rejected if they "do not provide significant value". Simple 2D icon packs are weak there.
    - **Sources:** [Unity submission guidelines](https://assetstore.unity.com/publishing/submission-guidelines), [Unity content transparency](https://unity.com/legal/asset-store-content-transparency).

21. **3D-printable gothic models** (OpenSCAD/CadQuery ornaments and cookie cutters) on Cults3D.
    - **Kept, but scored low.**
    - **Platforms:**
      - Cults3D pays creators 80%.
      - MakerWorld allows AI-generated content (AIGC) with a tag but **excludes AIGC from its cash Exclusive program**.
      - MyMiniFactory and Thingiverse are moving anti-AI (blog-sourced).
    - **Fit:** we cannot test-print, and there is a printability-complaint risk.
    - **Sources:** [payrequest (blog) on Cults fees](https://payrequest.io/blog/best-platforms-sell-3d-printing-files-stl), [Bambu forum on MakerWorld policy](https://forum.bambulab.com/t/the-death-of-exclusive-program-anti-ai-generated-models-policy/254093), [bambuhub (blog)](https://bambuhub.net/guides/makerworld-points-rewards).

22. **Envato / ThemeForest.** **Excluded** (bans AI-generated items).

23. **Figma Community paid files.** **Excluded** (not approving new sellers of paid files).

24. **Gumroad as a second storefront.** Not a separate venture. Its fees are 10% + $0.50 direct and 30% via Discover ([checkoutpage](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/gumroad-fees)), worse than Payhip, which report A already covered. Use it only for Discover exposure.

## 5. Commonly hyped, not recommended

1. **AI niche blogs and programmatic SEO for AdSense or affiliate income.**
   - This is the most-sold "passive income" plan, and it now mostly fails.
   - Google names AI page generation without added value as scaled content abuse and enforced it in the Aug 2026 spam update.
   - AI Overviews cut clicks roughly in half where shown (Pew).
   - AdSense rejects "low value content".
   - Raptive requires "meaningful human involvement".
   - A small, genuinely useful tool site (B2) is the only version I recommend.
2. **Faceless AI YouTube and TikTok channels for ad or creator-fund money.**
   - YouTube's July 2025 "inauthentic content" policy demonetizes interchangeable, mass-produced videos.
   - TikTok's Creator Rewards excludes fully AI-generated videos.
   - Both still need 1k+ or 10k+ followers first.
   - Using video only as traffic to the shop is allowed but weak.
3. **AI music uploads to Spotify and streaming.** Spotify removed more than 75M spam tracks and filters mass uploads.
4. **AI articles on Medium.** Banned from the Partner Program paywall.
5. **AI asset packs on Envato, ThemeForest or Elements.** AI-generated items are banned outright.
6. **"Ship 50 template apps" on the app stores.** Apple rejects template and spam apps (4.2.6/4.3), and new Google Play personal accounts need 12 real testers for 14 days.
7. **Scraped-data products and directories.** These break the source sites' terms and resell others' content.
8. **AI-run "paid communities" or courses.** They need a real host. Running one without disclosure is deceptive.
9. **AI newsletters "monetized by sponsors".** Sponsor networks start at around 10k subscribers (Kit). Without an existing audience this takes a year or more, and beehiiv's monetization tier breaks the $100 budget.
