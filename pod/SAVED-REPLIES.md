# Saved replies for print-on-demand orders (paste into Etsy messages)

**Where is my order?**
Thank you for your order! Each item is printed for you after you order, which takes 2–5 business days, and then it ships. You'll get a tracking number by email as soon as it's on its way. Let me know if anything looks off.

**My item arrived damaged / misprinted**
I'm so sorry! Could you send a photo of the problem? I'll have a replacement printed and shipped to you at no cost.

**Can I change the size/colour or address?**
I'll do my best. Orders go to print quickly, so please message me right away with the change and I'll check whether it's still possible.
