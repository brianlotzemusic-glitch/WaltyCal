Instrument Families and CareLesson 1 · Instrument families, how sound is made, and caring for your instrument
LESSON 1
Sub plan · no instruments
GoalStudents read about the four instrument families, sort instruments into them and learn the basic rules for caring for an instrument.
You needPencils and the reading and worksheet (page 6, page 7). No instruments, no audio, no screens.
Timeabout 42 min
for band or orchestra
Step by step ·Italic lines are words you can say.
  1. 5 minStep 1
    Welcome
    Say: “Good morning, musicians! I’m your guest teacher today. Your director left a plan, and today is a no-instruments day, so please leave your instruments in their cases or in storage.” Take attendance the way the welcome sheet (page 3) says. Hand out pencils.
  2. 5 minStep 2
    Warm-up question
    Write on the board: Which instrument do you play, and is it a woodwind, brass, percussion or string instrument? Take four or five answers. It is fine if they are not sure; that is what today is for.
  3. 15 minStep 3
    Read together
    Hand out the reading (page 6). Students read one family at a time, one volunteer per paragraph. After each family ask: What do players of this family do to start the sound? The care rules at the bottom are read by the whole class.
  4. 12 minStep 4
    Worksheet
    Hand out the worksheet (page 7). Students work alone; they may look back at the reading. Walk around and point students to the reading, not to the answer. The answers are on page 24.
  5. 5 minStep 5
    Check and share
    Read the answers aloud from page 24 and have students check their own work in a different color. Last, ask pairs to share one care rule they will follow this week.
Extra time? Play Name That Family (page 4) or hand out the early finishers page (page 23). Short class? Do steps 1, 3 and 4, and send the worksheet home to finish.
