Emergency sub plans for band and orchestra, grades 6–12, that a substitute with no music background can run. Five complete lessons, each with a plan page (timed steps, words the sub can read aloud, materials), a reading or worksheet, and a printed answer key. No instruments, no audio and no screens are needed, so the plans work in a band room, an orchestra room, a mixed class or any classroom.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (29 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover and teacher notes: 2 pages (this page and how to use the packet)
• For the substitute: 2 pages (welcome sheet, lessons at a glance, game bank)
• Lesson 1 · Instrument Families and Care: 3 pages (plan, reading, sort and match)
• Lesson 2 · Rhythm and Note-Value Math: 4 pages (plan and three engraved-rhythm worksheets)
• Lesson 3 · Composers and Music History: 3 pages (plan, reading, questions and timeline math)
• Lesson 4 · Practice Journal & Reflection: 4 pages (plan, strategies, journal, reflection)
• Lesson 5 · Seating and Terminology Puzzle: 4 pages (plan, seating charts, terms, word search)
• Early finishers: 1 page (scramble, riddles, concert program)
• Answer keys: 4 pages (every lesson, with the word search solution)
• Sub report: 1 page (notes back to you)
• Terms of use: 1 page (license and credits)
Total: 29 pages

THE FIVE LESSONS (each about 40–45 minutes)
• Lesson 1, Instrument Families and Care: instrument families, how sound is made, and caring for your instrument
• Lesson 2, Rhythm and Note-Value Math: note values, time signatures and beats as math
• Lesson 3, Composers and Music History: a reading about four composers, with comprehension questions
• Lesson 4, Practice Journal & Reflection: practice strategies, goal setting and honest reflection
• Lesson 5, Seating and Terminology Puzzle: where the sections sit, and the Italian terms in your music

WHY IT WORKS FOR SUBS
• Every plan has timed steps and “say this” scripts, so a sub can run it cold
• Instruments stay in their cases: no playing, no equipment, no recordings
• Lessons work in any order and each stands alone, so a multi-day absence is covered
• Welcome sheet to fill in once, a game bank of 6 no-prep games, and a sub report for notes back to you
• Each lesson has a short-class option and an extra-time option

ACCURATE BY DESIGN
• Rhythms are engraved with a professional music font in 4/4; every rhythm was read back by computer and every answer in the keys was computed from the same data
• The composer reading uses only well-documented facts, and era dates are marked as rough
• The word search is solved by computer: every word is in the grid exactly once
• Seating charts show the common layout, and the page tells students their own ensemble may differ

• Grades 6–12; works for band, orchestra, jazz band or mixed ensemble classes
• Prints well in black and white
• Nothing seasonal, so it works any week of the year
• All lessons, readings, puzzles and art are original. No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
