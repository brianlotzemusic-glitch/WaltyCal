Roll and ComposeShare and perform
SHARE
Performance ideas
NameDate
Every composition deserves a performance. Pick one idea for each lesson, or let students choose their own way to share.
1Clap and say

Clap the rhythm while saying ta, ti-ti and shh. Count 1 2 3 4 out loud before you start.

2Body percussion

Pat, clap, snap or stomp the rhythm. Use a different sound for each symbol in Level A.

3Classroom percussion

Play the rhythm on drums, wood blocks or shakers. Rests stay silent.

4Bells and keyboards

Set up C, D, E, G and A. On xylophones and bells, lift off the F and B bars.

5Call and response

One player performs the call (bars 1⁠–⁠4). A partner answers with the response (bars 5⁠–⁠8).

6Swap and play

Trade sheets with a partner and perform each other's compositions.

7Gallery walk

Post the compositions. Classmates read them and leave a star and a wish on a sticky note.

8Class piece

Join four 4-bar compositions end to end. Play them in order as a class piece.

9Add an ostinato

A partner plays a short pattern that repeats over and over while you perform your piece.

10Record and listen

Record each performance on a tablet or phone. Listen back and talk about what worked well.

A sharing routine in three steps
1Introduce

Say your title. Tell the class which dice rolls you used.

2Perform

Count in 1 2 3 4. Play or say your piece with a steady beat.

3Respond

Listeners name one star and one wish. Then fill in the rubric on page 14.

Tip for teachers. Let students choose whether to play or say their piece. Keep each performance short, and ask listeners to tap the beat quietly. Celebrate every composition: the dice make each one different.
