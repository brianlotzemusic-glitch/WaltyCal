Turkey Rhythm ReadingLesson 1 · Reading and clapping rhythms · no tech, no prep
LESSON 1
Sub plan
GoalStudents clap, say and read rhythms with ta, ti-ti and shh (grades 3–5 add ta-a and ta-a-a-a).
You needPencils, the rhythm chart (page 5) shown or copied on the board, and the worksheets.
CopiesK–2: page 7
3–5: page 8
about 45 min
Step by step ·Italic lines are words you can say.
  1. 5 minStep 1
    Turkey trot echo
    Say: “Happy November, musicians! I’m your guest teacher today. Let’s warm up. Echo me!” Say each line and let the class repeat it: Ears are on. Eyes are up. Hands are still. Let’s make music! Then pat a slow, steady beat on your knees and have the class pat along.
  2. 10 minStep 2
    Learn the rhythm words
    Show the rhythm chart (page 5). Point to the key at the bottom: ta is one clap, ti-ti is two quick claps, shh is a silent beat (open hands, no sound). Say line 1 aloud, then clap it. The class echoes. Do lines 1 to 4 with every class. Grades 3–5 also try lines 5 to 8, which add ta-a (a long sound, 2 beats) and ta-a-a-a (4 beats).
  3. 10 minStep 3
    Gobble and freeze
    Pick one chart line as the gobble line. Clap lines in any order. The class echoes every line, but when the gobble line comes they stay silent and gobble quietly with their hands over their mouths. Let a student leader choose the lines for the last round.
  4. 15 minStep 4
    Worksheets
    Hand out the worksheet: K–2 Feather Rhythms (page 7), 3–5 Turkey Rhythm Math (page 8). Read the directions aloud. Clap each rhythm out loud with the class first if students ask. Answers are on page 16.
  5. 5 minStep 5
    Pass the pie
    Sit in a circle or stay in rows. Clap a short rhythm to the first student, who claps it to the next, and so on. Does it arrive the same? Ask: Which rhythm was the hardest to pass?
Extra time? Students make up a 4-beat rhythm of their own with pie, corn, tur-key and shh, then clap it for a neighbor. Or use the early finishers page (page 15). Short class? Do steps 1 to 3 and send the worksheet home to finish.
