## 2. Description

```
Thanksgiving-week music sub plan for grades K–5 that any substitute can teach, even with no music background. Three no-prep lessons, each with a step-by-step plan with timed steps and words to say, plus two worksheets: one for grades K–2 and one for grades 3–5. Every rhythm has its syllables (ta, ti-ti, shh, ta-a) printed under the notes, so the sub can lead echo clapping without reading music.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (18 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover and teacher notes with a page guide and sub-tub setup steps (2 pages)
• Welcome sheet to fill in for your sub, including your listening music (1 page)
• Lessons at a glance plus a game bank of 6 no-prep games (1 page)
• Turkey rhythm chart: 8 echo lines with syllables under every note (1 page)
• 3 sub plans, one per lesson (3 pages)
• 6 worksheets: a K–2 and a 3–5 sheet for each lesson (6 pages)
• Early finishers page: thankful turkey, rhythm and music words (1 page)
• Answer key for Lesson 1 and the example verse (1 page)
• Sub report form for notes back to you (1 page)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)

THE THREE LESSONS
• Lesson 1, Turkey Rhythm Reading: K–2 count the claps on feather rhythms and match word rhythms (pie, gob-ble, shh); 3–5 rhythm math, spot the bars that are not 4 beats, write the syllables
• Lesson 2, Gratitude Lyrics: K–2 chant "Thank you for ___" with a one- or two-syllable word and draw its rhythm; 3–5 write a four-line rhyming verse of 7 syllables a line that fits the beat
• Lesson 3, Thankful Listening: a listening response sheet. K–2 draw and circle loud/soft, fast/slow, happy/calm; 3–5 tempo, a dynamics graph, instrument families, mood words and a short explanation. Use any calm instrumental piece you choose (no audio included; a no-tech sound show is the backup)

WHY IT WORKS FOR SUBS
• No tech, no instruments and no music reading needed, apart from playing one piece of music in Lesson 3
• Each lesson is one 30–45 minute class, and the lessons work in any order
• Original games: Turkey Trot Echo, Gobble and Freeze, Pass the Pie, Thankful Circle, Silent Conductor, Feast Freeze
• Inclusive by design: about gratitude, food and music, with no history lesson, and students can pass or pick their own thankful word

ACCURATE BY DESIGN
Every rhythm is engraved with a professional music font and was checked by computer: each bar holds 4 beats (except the bars students must spot as wrong), the example lyric lines are counted by computer, and the answer key comes from the same checked data.

• Grades K–5, with separate K–2 and 3–5 worksheets every lesson
• Quarter notes, eighth-note pairs, quarter rests, half notes and whole notes in 4/4
• Prints well in black and white
• All lessons, chants, verses, games, words and art are original. No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
