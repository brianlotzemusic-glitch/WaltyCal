## 2. Description

```
Color by Music Symbol with three levels in one set, so every student in your class can color the same winter picture at their own level. 9 original, kid-friendly winter pictures, 27 worksheets and 27 full-color answer keys. Students read a dynamic marking, a tempo term or a music symbol in each space, find its meaning in the key, and color. Winter themed and non-denominational: it fits every school calendar.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (58 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover — 1 page
• Teacher notes with a level guide and page list — 1 page
• Student reference page: every dynamic, tempo term and music symbol with its meaning — 1 page
• Level A: Dynamics (pp, p, mp, mf, f, ff), 9 worksheets + 9 answer keys (grades 2–3) — 18 pages
• Level B: Tempo terms (largo, adagio, andante, moderato, allegro, presto), 9 worksheets + 9 answer keys (grades 3–5) — 18 pages
• Level C: Music symbols (fermata, repeat sign, crescendo, decrescendo, staccato, accent, slur), 9 worksheets + 9 answer keys (grades 4–5) — 18 pages
• Terms of use and credits — 1 page
Total: 58 pages.

PICTURES: Snowman, Mittens, Penguin, Cocoa Mug, Igloo, Snowflake, Snowy Pine Tree, Winter Hat, Ice Skate.

HOW TO USE
Print, hand out crayons and let students work at their own level. Levels A and B show the marking in each space and the key gives its meaning (for example, "very soft" or "fast"); Level C shows the symbol and the key gives its name. Each worksheet has a matching full-color answer key, and every key is generated from the same data as its worksheet and verified in code. Great for early finishers, music centers, sub plans and winter concert season.

• Grades 2–5 (choose the level that fits)
• Print in black and white: every color in the key is also written as a word
• No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
