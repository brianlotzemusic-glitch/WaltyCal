Three days instead of four

My Teacher Notes run this unit in 3 days: Day 1 as here; Day 2 plans and builds, adds the chord layer and starts the transitions; Day 3 gives 20 minutes to finish and submit, then presentations. The four-day plan keeps the same blocks and gives building its own day.

Section DifferentiationAll sections sound the same. No meaningful difference in texture or elements between sections.Some difference between sections but verse and chorus are not clearly distinct. Layering absent or ineffective.Verse and chorus clearly different. At least one chord layer added on a second instrument. Melody placement is intentional.Multiple sections have distinct textures. Addition and removal of elements is deliberate. Listener can identify the section by ear without looking at the screen.
Arrangement as MixNo evidence of mix decisions tied to arrangement.
