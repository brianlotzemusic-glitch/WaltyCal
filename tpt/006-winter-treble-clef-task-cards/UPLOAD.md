```
Winter treble clef note-reading practice for grades 2–5 music: 72 printable task cards in three levels, from single notes on the staff to ledger-line word puzzles, with a student reference page, recording sheets, worksheets and full answer keys. Snowflake, mitten and knit-hat art; nothing tied to one celebration, so it works in any school all winter.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (23 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover and teacher notes with a page guide and ways to use the cards (2 pages)
• Student reference: the treble staff from low C to high A, line notes, space notes, and the notes just outside the staff (1 page)
• 72 task cards, 8 per page, in three levels (9 pages)
• Recording sheets, one per level (3 pages)
• Worksheets, one per level (3 pages)
• Answer keys: all 72 cards on one page, plus a key for each worksheet (4 pages)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)

THREE LEVELS
• Level A (A1–A24): one note on the staff, E to F. Three answer choices on each card, so they double as clip cards
• Level B (B1–B24): three notes per card, adding the D below and the G above the staff
• Level C (C1–C24): name 3 to 7 notes to spell a word (BAG, FACE, CABBAGE and more), adding low C and high A on ledger lines

WORKSHEETS
• Worksheet A: name 16 notes and circle LINE or SPACE
• Worksheet B: name 8 three-note groups, then the line notes and space notes in order
• Worksheet C: a snowy word decoder with 10 words

WAYS TO USE THEM
Scoot or around the room, clip cards, centers, partners, early finishers, warm-ups under a document camera and sub days.

ACCURATE BY DESIGN
Every note is engraved with a professional music font, with correct stems and ledger lines. Each note's position on the staff was checked by computer against its answer, and every answer key comes from the same checked data.

• Grades 2–5
• Treble clef only, quarter notes, C4 to A5
• Prints well in black and white; print the cards on card stock
• All cards, words and art are original. No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters

Designed with the help of digital and AI tools, and checked by hand.
```
