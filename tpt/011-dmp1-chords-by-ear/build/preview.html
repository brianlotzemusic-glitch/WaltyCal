Remember: Roman numerals describe chord function, not just chord names.

The I chord always feels like home. · The V chord always feels unresolved. · The vi chord always feels tender or melancholy.

These feelings are the same in every key — only the note names change.

No sharps or flats in this key: every chord is white keys only. A minor uses the same seven notes: it is the relative minor.
