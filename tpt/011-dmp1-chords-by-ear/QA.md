## 2. Music theory: independent check

All of these are correct:
- Every spelling: C E G, D F A, E G B, F A C, G B D, A C E, B D F (C major); A C E, B D F, C E G, D F A, E G B, F A C, G B D (A minor). Roman numerals and case are right: C major I ii iii IV V vi vii°; A natural minor i ii° III iv v VI VII. Quality words (Major/Minor/Dim.) are right everywhere.
- Key signatures: C major and A minor have no sharps or flats; the cards say so and call A minor the relative minor and C major the relative major. Scale formulas are right: major WWHWWWH, natural minor WHWWHWW, major pentatonic W-W-WH-W-WH giving C D E G A, missing F and B. The reference sheet's minor pentatonic, harmonic minor, melodic minor (ascending), blues and chord-type formulas (aug, 7, maj7, m7) all add to 12 half steps and spell correctly.
- Chord formulas R-4-3, R-3-4, R-3-3; counting example (C# 1, D 2, D# 3, E 4, then F 1, F# 2, G 3). Answer key page 32: D major D F# A, B minor B D F#, D diminished D F Ab (3 + 3, correctly Ab not G#).
- Progressions: I-IV-V-I = C F G C; i-VI-III-VII = Am F C G; I-vi-IV-V = C Am F G. Feel Log Loop 1 and Loop 2 are these chords. The "same four chords, two homes" claim on page 34 is true as a set (C, Am, F, G) though the order differs, which the page states. Piano roll on page 34: I C4 E4 G4, vi A3 C4 E4, IV F3 A3 C4, V G3 B3 D4, with bass C2 A1 F1 G1. All rows match their labels.
- Songs: Twist and Shout (three chords, I-IV-V in D, so "a different key" is true); Let It Be I-V-vi-IV in C (C G Am F), with vi = A minor. Correct.
- Pacing minute blocks add to 53 on every day.

**The "white keys only" rule vs the D diminished box.** The packet (page 20) has D major (F#), B minor (F#) and D diminished (F, Ab), which are not white-key chords, while pages 2, 3, 23 and 24 and the description say "white keys only". The rule is true for the two keys used. Only the answer key (page 32) says "D major and B minor use a black key, D diminished uses A-flat". Student page 20 and the teacher notes say nothing, so a teacher or student will find those three boxes contradict the rule. Not wrong, but required: add one line to page 20 or page 11/12 (for example "D major, B minor and D diminished need black keys: they are for practising the formula, not for your key") and keep the key line.

**A minor Reference Card page 24, closing box.** It repeats the C major card text word for word: "The I chord always feels like home. The V chord always feels unresolved. The vi chord always feels tender or melancholy." The A minor card has no I, V or vi: its numerals are i, v and VI, and its own v row says "Less forceful tension than major V". A student working in A minor is told to look for chords that are not on their card. (This is the owner's Drive card; the owner's text is the same, but the kit prints it as the reference.) Fix: on the A minor card (page 24) use the minor wording: "The i chord always feels like home. The v or VII pulls back toward it. The VI chord feels warm and open." Keep the final line "These feelings are the same in every key — only the note names change" if it still fits. The blank card (page 25) already has a correct footnote about crossing out the major numerals.
