## 2. Description

```
Note Naming Worksheets: Treble, Bass & Alto Clef Speed Drills, Grades 3-8: 30 one-minute note naming drills, 10 each for treble clef, bass clef and alto clef. Each drill is a grid of 30 single notes; students write the letter name under every note while the timer runs, then check with the answer key and colour their progress chart. Quick, low-prep practice for general music, band, orchestra and choir in grades 3–8.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (43 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover (1 page)
• Teacher notes with the ten levels and where to start (1 page)
• Student guide: how to drill, scoring and tricks (1 page)
• Clef reference: every note in treble, bass and alto clef with its name (1 page)
• Progress chart (bar graph for all 30 drills) (1 page)
• Drill log (three tries per drill) (1 page)
• Treble clef drills 1–10, 30 notes each (10 pages)
• Bass clef drills 1–10, 30 notes each (10 pages)
• Alto clef drills 1–10, 30 notes each (10 pages)
• Answer keys (5 drills per page, item by item) (6 pages)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)

THE TEN LEVELS (the same ladder in every clef)
1. Lines  2. Spaces  3. Lines and spaces  4. Just above and below the staff  5. One ledger line  6. Two ledger lines  7. Three ledger lines  8. Sharps and flats  9. Sharps, flats and ledger lines  10. Mixed challenge

HOW TO USE
Set a one-minute timer, students name as many notes as they can, then count their correct answers (out of 30) and shade a bar on the progress chart. Repeat any drill for a new personal best. Great as a bell ringer, warm-up, homework, sub-plan filler or station. Alto clef is drawn correctly, with the C clef centred on the middle line (middle C), so violists and anyone learning to read it get real practice.

• Grades 3–8 (treble from grade 3; bass and alto for older beginners and orchestra)
• 30 notes per drill, new notes on every page; answer keys for every drill
• Print in black and white; the answer keys use one accent color
• Letter names only (no octave numbers); no songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
