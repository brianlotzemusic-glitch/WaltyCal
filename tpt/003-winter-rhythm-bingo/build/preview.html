Reading a pattern · every square is one 4-beat measure

1 e & a 2 3 (4) &

Four sixteenths (1 beat) + a quarter note (1 beat) + a dotted quarter and an eighth (2 beats) = 4 beats.

  • Count with the beat number, then e & a for sixteenths and & for eighths.
  • Numbers in (parentheses) are beats you hold or rest: keep counting in your head.
  • A dot adds half the note’s value: a dotted half is 2 + 1 = 3 beats; a dotted quarter is 1 + ½ = 1½ beats.
  • One beam means eighth notes; two beams mean sixteenth notes.
