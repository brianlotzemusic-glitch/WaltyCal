## 2. Description

```
Winter rhythm bingo for grades 3–6 music: you clap or play a 4-beat rhythm, and students read their cards to find it. 30 unique bingo cards, 36 rhythm calling cards with counts, and a caller's checklist, all with snowflake, mitten, sled and cocoa art. No holiday or religious images, so it works all winter long, December through February.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (40 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Teacher notes and How to Play: prep, ways to call, game variations, tips (2 pages)
• Rhythm reference / anchor chart: all 9 rhythm values with beats, counts and optional syllables (1 page)
• Caller's checklist: all 36 patterns with counts, to tick as you call and to check winners (1 page)
• 30 unique bingo cards, one per page, each with 24 rhythm patterns and a FREE snowflake centre (30 pages)
• 36 calling cards, 12 per page, each with the pattern in 4/4, the counts (1 e & a) and optional syllables (3 pages)
• Snowflake and winter bingo markers (1 page)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)

THE RHYTHMS
• #1–12: quarter notes, eighth-note pairs, quarter rests, half notes
• #13–24: four sixteenths, eighth + two sixteenths, two sixteenths + eighth
• #25–36: dotted quarter + eighth, dotted half note
Every pattern is exactly one 4-beat measure in 4/4, written in standard notation on a one-line rhythm staff.

FAIR, UNIQUE CARDS
Every card holds 24 of the 36 patterns, and each pattern appears on exactly 20 cards. No two cards are alike, and no two cards share a winning row, column or diagonal, so two students can't win on the very same line.

HOW TO PLAY
Draw a calling card and perform it: clap it, play it on a drum or one pitch, or speak the counts. Students find the rhythm and cover it. Five in a row wins, and the winner performs their line back to you. Variations: four corners, X, frame, two lines, blackout, "mystery count" and a compose-your-own extension.

• Grades 3–6
• Counts on every calling card; Kodály-style syllables are optional and in small print, so use any system your students know
• Prints well in black and white
• No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters

Designed with the help of digital and AI tools, and checked by hand.
```
