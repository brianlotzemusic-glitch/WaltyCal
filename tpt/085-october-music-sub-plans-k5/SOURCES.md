## Rules followed
Respectful, secular and inclusive wording; short facts only; no speech quotes, no lyrics, no recordings and no named songs, albums or groups; no stereotypes (Day of the Dead is never shown as a costume or a decoration); no guessing of students' backgrounds; Halloween and Day of the Dead optional and swappable; a sensitive-wording note for a student who has lost someone; mention of the hemisphere wherever a season is stated; no technology, singing, performing or music reading by the sub.
