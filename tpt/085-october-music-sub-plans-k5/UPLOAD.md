## 2. Description

```
Need sub plans a substitute can teach without any music background, any technology, or any prep from you? These eight October music days are ready to print and hand over. Each day has a step-by-step plan with timed steps and words to say, plus two student sheets: one for grades K–2 and one for grades 3–5. The theme is October: the fall harvest and the sounds of fall, friendly and silly Halloween, and Day of the Dead (Día de Muertos), a short, respectful note on the Mexican tradition of remembering loved ones, with teacher notes on teaching it with care. Halloween and Day of the Dead are both optional: every sheet that mentions Halloween has a harvest choice, so the days work for every family.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (48 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover and teacher notes with the October note, Halloween and Day of the Dead teaching notes, setup steps and a page guide (2 pages)
• Sub quick start, welcome sheet, days at a glance, quick games, word-rhythm chart, October facts and a class sign (7 pages)
• 8 sub plans, one per day, each 30–45 minutes (8 pages)
• 16 student sheets: a K–2 and a 3–5 sheet for each day (16 pages)
• Day extras: movement picture cards, chant word cards, a chant book page and a read-aloud quiz (4 pages)
• Early finishers (2 pages)
• Exit tickets (1 page)
• Answer keys for Days 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8; Day 3 is open-ended and its entry says what to look for (3 pages)
• Sub report form, folder labels, class star awards, terms of use and a thank-you page (5 pages)

THE EIGHT DAYS
• Day 1, October Lookout: K–2 hear a short reading about the fall harvest, Halloween and Day of the Dead and circle answers; 3–5 read a passage of about 200 words and answer five questions
• Day 2, Crunch and Clap: clap the syllables of October words and a short original four-line poem; K–2 color a circle for each clap, 3–5 sort words by syllables and build a four-beat chant
• Day 3, Fall Sound Scenes: meet five sounds of fall (rustle, crunch, thump, whoo, boom), then draw a fall scene and label the sounds in it (paper only); K–2 label two sounds, 3–5 label three and write two sentences
• Day 4, Float, Stomp and Freeze: move and freeze to a tapped beat, try call and response, with 8 picture cards (pumpkin, apple, corn, leaf, marigold, drum, bat, ghost); K–2 circle how they moved, 3–5 reflect and design a freeze game
• Day 5, October Puzzle Patch: fall word puzzles; K–2 match words to pictures and trace, 3–5 word search plus a decode-the-message puzzle
• Day 6, Fall Festival Crew: music jobs at a fall festival: five music jobs, from conductor to radio host; K–2 draw a job, 3–5 match jobs and write a job ad or interview questions
• Day 7, My Harvest Chant Book: build four-beat chants from October word cards, mark the syllables, share with a partner, and keep a chant book page
• Day 8, Pumpkin Patch Bingo: bingo (12 squares for K–2, 16 for 3–5), a read-aloud quiz and Pass the Pencil, with a look back at the month

WHY IT WORKS FOR SUBS
• No tech: nothing needs a computer, projector, speaker or video. Paper, pencils, crayons, hands and voices only
• No music skills: rhythm is taught through speech rhythm, clapping the syllables of words, with the claps printed as dots. The sub never reads music or sings
• No prep: print, put each day in a folder, and go. Each day works in any order for a mixed K–5 class
• Facts are short, sourced and secular, and the facts page answers common questions, so the days work in public schools and for students of every background
```
