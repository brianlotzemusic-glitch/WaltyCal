## 2. Description

```
Sound design and automation for high school music technology: students design a snowstorm from scratch in Logic Pro, with no melody and no audio supplied. They build six or more layers of wind, ice, rumble, pads and noise with Alchemy, Quick Sampler and Retro Synth, shape a build, peak and fade of 60 to 90 seconds with automation of filter cutoff, volume, pan and reverb send, and place every layer with reverb, EQ and stereo width. This Winter Soundscape project takes 4 class periods and is an original Hudson Beat project; the steps are written for Logic Pro, while the ideas (layers, automation, a build, peak and fade) carry over to GarageBand and BandLab.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (ZIP: 28-page PDF = 1 cover + 9 teacher + 17 student + 1 terms page, plus 15-slide intro deck as .pptx and PDF)
• Teacher pages: how to use, project overview, setup, a 4-class pacing guide with the deck slides for each class, two pages of teacher notes (struggles, variations, resources, grading), a Logic quick reference, and a two-page worked example (an invented plan on paper: a 60-second storm at 120 BPM with a bar and time table, a 30-bar map, eight layers and four automation lanes) (9 pages)
• Student pages: a scene brief with three scenes and a scene card, eight sound-palette cards (what each layer does, how to make it in words, and its frequency zone), Alchemy, Quick Sampler and Retro Synth recipes, a two-page automation guide with curve diagrams, a reverb, EQ and stereo width page with a frequency map, a storm-arc and timeline planner with a length helper, a layer map, an automation plan, the 3-page project sheet (overview, goals, vocabulary, requirements, steps, tips, submission checklist), the /25 rubric, a vocabulary page, a feedback planner and a reflection page (17 pages)
• Teacher-presented intro deck, 15 slides with speaker notes on every slide: a think-and-share hook, what the project is, the storm arc, building the layers, a frequency map, automation curves, a worked-example map, space and mix, what to turn in and the rubric

THE FOUR CLASS PERIODS (works in 3 or 4 classes)
• Class 1: hook; choose a scene; plan the storm arc and its timings; start the first layers
• Class 2: build the layers in Alchemy, Quick Sampler and Retro Synth
• Class 3: automate four parameters; add reverb, EQ and stereo width; mix
• Class 4: bounce and hand in; presentations with feedback questions; reflection

WHAT STUDENTS LEARN
• Designing layers of sound from noise, pads and short samples, each with its own job and its own range of pitch
• Planning a timeline in bars and seconds (seconds = bars × 240 ÷ BPM) so the piece lasts 60 to 90 seconds
• Drawing and recording automation (filter cutoff, volume, pan, reverb send) so a sound moves and breathes
• Placing sounds with ChromaVerb or Space Designer, Channel EQ and stereo width, and keeping the loudest moment clean
• Explaining their choices in a presentation and a reflection

GOOD TO KNOW
• There is no audio in this resource, no melody to enter and no student work. Students make every sound themselves in Logic Pro
• The theme is winter weather, not a holiday: no carols, lyrics, characters or brands
• Requires Logic Pro on a Mac. A microphone and a MIDI keyboard are optional; nobody has to read music
• Rubric: 5 categories scored 1-5, /25; the final grade is the average of the student's and the teacher's score
• Grades 9-12; music technology, music production or sound design classes
• The deck uses the free Oswald and Source Sans 3 fonts (built into Google Slides); install them for PowerPoint or Keynote, or present the PDF copy, which shows the intended look
• Every student page prints well in black and white

Logic Pro is a product of Apple Inc., named only to describe compatibility. This resource is not affiliated with Apple.

Created with the help of digital and AI tools.
```
