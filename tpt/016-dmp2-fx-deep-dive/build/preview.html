The one rule for Class 2

From my common struggles: do the EQ demo early, with dramatic cuts and boosts on a soloed track. Slide 8 is that demo, and the EQ guide (page 10) is your script.

Classes 3–5

Working days. Slides 12 and 13 open Classes 3 and 4; Class 5 has no new slide. Circulate and ask: why is that effect there, and what happens when you bypass it? For my mix critique day, use the end of Class 5 or Class 6 to play two or three volunteer mixes on the main speakers.

Requirements ·

Choose your source: remix your own previous project OR pick a multitrack from Cambridge-MT
Use at least 5 different FX from the 11 covered in your class notes
Every track in your mix must be EQ’d and panned thoughtfully
FX choices should be stylistically appropriate for the genre
Your final mix should demonstrate clarity, depth, and balance

Steps / Process ·

  1. 1
    Review — Revisit your notes on the 11 FX from the Native Instruments video (notes page: page 14)
  2. 2
    Choose your source — Decide whether to remix your own project or download a multitrack from Cambridge-MT (log it on page 13)
  3. 3
    Plan and apply FX — Add at least 5 different effects across your mix. Think about genre conventions. (FX log: page 16)
  4. 4
    EQ and pan every track — Shape tone with EQ and place each instrument in the stereo field (Track map: page 15)
  5. 5
    Refine your mix — Adjust levels, tweak FX settings, and check on headphones and speakers
  6. 6
    Finalize — Bounce your final mix and prepare to walk the class through your FX choices
  7. 7
    Present & Get Feedback — Create your own Padlet post with 3 questions you want classmates to answer about your project. Present to the class. During classmates’ presentations, answer at least 2 of their 3 questions on their Padlet post.
