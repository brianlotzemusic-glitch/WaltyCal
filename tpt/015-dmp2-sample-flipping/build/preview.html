Check your time

Slice length = sample length ÷ number of slices. At your tempo, how many beats is one slice? (One beat = 60 ÷ BPM seconds.)

Requirements ·

Choose one sample from the provided options (my class set has 50; page 4 shows how to build yours)
Use Quick Sampler to manipulate and transform the sample
Key and tempo are up to you — make sure they fit the sample
Drum patterns must be self-created
Use layers appropriate to the style
Apply EQ and plug-ins to polish your mix

Steps / Process ·

  1. 1
    Listen and choose — Browse the provided samples and select one (log your choices on page 12)
  2. 2
    Learn Quick Sampler — Import your sample and experiment with slicing, pitching, and playback modes (reference: page 10)
  3. 3
    Build your beat — Program your own drum pattern that fits the style (page 14)
  4. 4
    Layer and arrange — Add bass, chords, melodies, and additional elements
  5. 5
    Mix — EQ, pan, and balance everything
  6. 6
    Finalize — Bounce your track and prepare your presentation
  7. 7
    Present & Get Feedback — Create your own Padlet post with 3 questions you want classmates to answer about your project. Present to the class. During classmates’ presentations, answer at least 2 of their 3 questions on their Padlet post.
