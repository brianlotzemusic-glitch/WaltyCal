REFERENCE
20 symbols
Treble clefMarks the higher notes. It curls around the G line.
Bass clefMarks the lower notes. Its two dots sit around the F line.
Quarter noteLasts 1 beat in 4/4.
Half noteLasts 2 beats in 4/4.
Whole noteLasts 4 beats in 4/4.
Quarter restSilence for 1 beat.
Half restSilence for 2 beats. It sits on the line.
Repeat signGo back and play the music again.
FermataHold the note longer than usual.
SharpRaises a note by a half step.
FlatLowers a note by a half step.
NaturalCancels a sharp or a flat.
Forte (f)Play loudly.
Piano (p)Play softly.
Mezzo forte (mf)Play medium loud.
Mezzo piano (mp)Play medium soft.
CrescendoGet gradually louder.
DecrescendoGet gradually softer.
Time signature 4/4Four beats in a bar. A quarter note gets one beat.
Double barlineMarks the end of a section.

In the scenes the symbols hide without a staff. Stems go up on the right of the head below the middle line, and down on the left on or above it.
