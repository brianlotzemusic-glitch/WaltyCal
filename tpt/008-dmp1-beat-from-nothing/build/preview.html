Project sheet

Digital Music Production 1 · Unit 1 · Structured Project · Drum Machine Designer

Overview ·

You’re going to build an original drum beat from nothing: no loops, no pre-made grooves. Starting with the three building blocks of a drum kit (kick, snare, hi-hat), you’ll explore rhythm, pattern, and what makes a beat feel right. You’ll also analyze a famous drum beat to understand how real producers think about groove. By the end, you’ll have made something entirely your own, and you’ll understand exactly why it works.

Goals ·

  • Identify the kick, snare, and hi-hat by name, sound, and function
  • Build an original drum beat using Logic Pro’s Drum Machine Designer
  • Understand how volume and panning affect the feel of a mix
  • Experience the creative process of building something musical from scratch

Requirements ·

Built entirely in Drum Machine Designer: no Apple Loops
Must include: kick drum, snare drum, closed hi-hat, and at least one additional element (open hi-hat, clap, rim, shaker, etc.)
Minimum 16 measures, structured in AABA form (A = main beat, B = variation, A = main beat returns)
Volume adjusted thoughtfully on all tracks: nothing at default
Panning applied intentionally to at least 2 tracks
Project named correctly: Your Name – Beat From Nothing
Exported as MP3 and .zip (compressed .logicx file) and submitted to your class page before presenting
Your beat’s shape

4 measures each · 16 measures · B = A with one change

Key vocabulary

14 terms on page 16: kick, snare, hi-hat, loop, beat, measure, step sequencer, subdivision, tempo, pattern region, cycle, AABA, volume, panning.
