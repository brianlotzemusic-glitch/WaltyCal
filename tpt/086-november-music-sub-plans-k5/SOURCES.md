### 5. Native nations have different histories, languages and music traditions, so there is no single Native music; Native nations are living communities today (Day 1 readings, facts page, teacher notes). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_American_music (fetched 26 Nov 2026: 'Different tribes have different traditions about their drums and how to play them'; 'Percussion, especially drums and rattles, are common accompaniment'); https://festival.si.edu/articles/1973/the-courting-flute-in-native-american-tradition (Smithsonian Folklife Festival, fetched 26 Nov 2026: the flute was 'Used principally by Woodlands tribes, Southwestern tribes and tribes from the Southern and Northern Plains'); https://www.bia.gov/faqs/what-federally-recognized-tribe (self-government). Independent QA (26 Nov 2026) also fetched https://pages.stolaf.edu/americanmusic/tag/rattles/ (rattles appear across North America, with meanings that differ by region and nation, and some are ceremonial or sacred: the bundle only says 'many Native nations use rattles in their music') and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Native_American_flute (the Native American flute has an external block, often bird-shaped, and is 'not strictly an end-blown flute'; the bundle therefore draws and describes only a generic flute and says 'Flutes come in many shapes'). Smithsonian NMAI pages, the Met essay on Plains instruments and Britannica could not be fetched (403 or 429), so Wikipedia is used for the drum and rattle statements and is worded minimally.

## Rules followed
Respectful, secular and inclusive wording; short facts only; no speech quotes, no lyrics, no recordings and no named songs, albums or groups; no stereotypes (no headdress, tipi, costume, regalia or "Native-style" decoration; Native nations are never one group and never in the past tense); no guessing of students' backgrounds; Thanksgiving optional and swappable; a note for students with a family member who served or died; mention of the hemisphere wherever a season is stated; no technology, singing, performing or music reading by the sub.
