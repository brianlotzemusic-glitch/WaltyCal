## 2. Curriculum structure per the owner's documents

DMP1 Restructure (doc 1HD0lJV5): **10 units, 60 days + 5 buffer, school year Sept-May.** Principles: feel it before you name it; build small before big; earn the loops. One mixing concept is woven into each unit. Day split shown as (intro/work/present).

| # | Unit | Days | When | Mixing concept | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Beat From Nothing | 4 | Sep | Volume and panning | Original drum beat (no loops), copy Billie Jean pattern then original |
| 2 | Stems & Grooves | 6 | Sep-Oct | Gain staging | Drums over a pro stem (AABA), Group 2 full-song drums with bus reverb |
| 3 | Chords by Ear | 5 | Oct | Balance | None (learning unit): hear I-IV-V-I, then KCSRN worksheet; C major/A minor |
| 4 | Bass & Melody Writing | 4 | Oct-Nov | Frequency separation | Bassline and short melody over given chords |
| 5 | Song Form | 4 | Nov | Arrangement as mix | 32-measure skeleton, then form-spreadsheet comparison |
| 6 | Spooky Tunes | 6 | Nov-Dec | Reverb/delay via bus | 32 measures scored to a woods video, two minor keys |
| 7 | Loops Unlocked | 6 | Dec | EQ intro | 24-measure Apple Loops arrangement |
| 8 | EDM / Full Song | 9 | Jan | Bussing and automation | 64-measure EDM track |
| 9 | Video Game + Sound Design | 9 | Feb-Mar | Compression | 1-min game score (max 2 loops) plus recorded SFX |
| 10 | Final Project | 7 | Apr-May | All, by choice | Student-pitched project, proposal form |

DAW assumed: **Logic Pro** (Drum Machine Designer, Step Sequencer, Space Designer/ChromaVerb, Apple Loops, Compressor), MPK Mini keyboard, Scarlett Solo interface, condenser mic. Also references Padlet for presentations, Spotify playlists, musictheory.net, cambridge-mt.com stems, SoundBible.

Mapping note: the Drive folders do not match the doc numbering. Drive has 6 built folders (1 Beat, 2 Stems, 3 Building Blocks, 4 Song Form, 5 Spooky, 6 Loops). "3 - The Building Blocks" (Keyboard, Scales & Melody, Chords & Roman Numerals, Basslines) is the new-format content for doc Units 3 and 4 together. EDM, Video Game + Sound Design and Final Project have no Drive folder in "DMP1 (New)" yet (EDM is only in the 2025 archive folder).

DMP2 (menu doc 1uQIgG_J): **16 projects, each 7 class periods, chosen in any order**; the Final is a class/team capstone. Types: Structured: Alchemy Architect (synthesis), Billboard Top 10 (song form and drums), FX Deep Dive (mixing, 5+ effects), Genre Exploration (research and style), Pattern Prodigy (step sequencer), Royalty Free (1-min functional piece from 3 words), Session Player's Studio (arrangement). No pacing order beyond "any order, 7 periods".
