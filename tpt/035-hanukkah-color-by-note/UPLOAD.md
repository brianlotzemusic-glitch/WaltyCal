Hanukkah Color by Note with three levels in one set, so every student in your class can color the same picture at their own reading level. 9 original, kid-friendly Hanukkah pictures, 27 worksheets and 27 full-color answer keys.

Students read the note or rest in each space, find it in the color key, and color the space. In levels B and C every space shows its note on a small staff with a clef, and the key uses letter names. Great for early finishers, music centers, sub plans and winter holiday lessons in December.
