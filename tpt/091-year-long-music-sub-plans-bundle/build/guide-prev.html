Welcome! This ZIP holds all 12 monthly Music Sub Plans bundles for grades K-5: a full school year, August through July. Each month has 8 sub days with a step-by-step plan, words to say, and two student sheets (one for grades K-2, one for grades 3-5). A substitute needs no music background, no technology and no prep from you.

How to use this bundle

  1. Unzip the download. Each month has its own numbered folder (01-August to 12-July) holding that month’s PDF.
  2. Look at the next two pages: the table lists every month with its theme and page count, and the calendar shows which month to open.
  3. Print the month you need, or the whole year. Each month’s PDF starts with a teacher page; the answer keys are inside the PDF.
  4. Put a day in a folder for your sub. Every day takes 30 to 45 minutes and works in any order, so you can use any day from any month when you are out.

What you saved

Each month is also sold separately

Every monthly bundle in this ZIP is also sold on its own in the Hudson Beat store on Teachers Pay Teachers, and the files are the same. If you already own some months, you have them twice: use the folders you need.

Good to know

  • Everything is paper and pencil: no computer, projector, speaker, recording or instrument is needed, and the sub never has to sing, play or read music.
  • Some months touch on holidays and heritage months with short, plain, respectful facts, and the optional ones are marked in each month’s teacher notes.
  • Every page prints well in black and white unless it says otherwise.
