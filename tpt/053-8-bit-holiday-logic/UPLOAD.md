## 2. Description

```
Chiptune music making for high school music technology: students arrange the public-domain carol “O Come, All Ye Faithful” as an 8-bit game tune in Logic Pro. They enter the melody from a piano-roll note chart on a square-wave lead, play the chords as arpeggios with the Arpeggiator, add a triangle-wave bass and a noise drum, and crush it with a Bitcrusher. This 8-Bit Holiday project takes 4 class periods and is an original Hudson Beat project; the steps are written for Logic Pro, while the ideas (square, triangle and noise channels, arpeggios and a lo-fi crush make a chiptune) carry over to GarageBand, Ableton Live and BandLab.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (ZIP: 27-page PDF = 1 cover + 10 teacher + 15 student + 1 terms page, plus 15-slide intro deck as .pptx and PDF)
• Teacher pages: how to use, project overview, setup, a 4-class pacing guide with the deck slides for each class, two pages of teacher notes (struggles, variations, resources, grading), a Logic quick reference, the tune's sources and the public-domain rule, and a two-page worked example (an invented plan on paper: the carol as a 66-second tune at 120 BPM, with its four channels, an intro, a 33-bar map and two automation lanes) (10 pages)
• Student pages: three piano-roll note-chart pages with every note listed bar by bar, the chords and a bass line for all 21 bars, a page on the four channels (square, pulse, triangle and noise) and the Bitcrusher, arpeggio cards (every chord of the carol spelled out, four arpeggio patterns), four noise drum patterns with grids, My plan, a Layer map with a length helper, the 3-page project sheet (overview, goals, vocabulary, requirements, steps, tips, submission checklist), the /25 rubric, a vocabulary page, a feedback planner and a reflection page (15 pages)
• Teacher-presented intro deck, 15 slides with speaker notes on every slide: a think-and-share hook, what the project is, how to read a piano roll, the four chip channels, arpeggios, building the sound, noise drum patterns, a worked-example map, what to turn in and the rubric

THE FOUR CLASS PERIODS
• Class 1: hook; read the chart; fill in My plan; enter the melody on a square-wave lead
• Class 2: build the channels: arpeggios with the Arpeggiator, a triangle bass and the chip sounds
• Class 3: add the noise drum and the Bitcrusher; plan the form and arrange 31 to 35 bars
• Class 4: bounce and hand in; presentations with feedback questions; reflection

WHAT STUDENTS LEARN
• Entering and editing notes in Logic Pro's Piano Roll from a note chart
• Spelling the chords of a key and playing them as arpeggios
• Why a game chip has square, triangle and noise channels that play one note at a time, and how to build them with a synthesizer
• Using a Bitcrusher and automation to make a lo-fi sound that changes from section to section
• Planning a 45 to 75 second arrangement in bars and seconds (bars × 240 ÷ BPM), then explaining their choices in a reflection

GOOD TO KNOW
• There is no audio in this resource and no student work. The carol is in the public domain (attributed to John Francis Wade, c. 1743; first published 1751) and appears only as a note chart, with no lyrics; students make every sound themselves in Logic Pro
• “O Come, All Ye Faithful” is a Christian Christmas carol; offer it where carols suit your school, or let students bring a public-domain tune you approve
• Requires Logic Pro on a Mac. A MIDI keyboard is not needed; nobody has to read staff notation
• Rubric: 5 categories scored 1-5, /25; the final grade is the average of the student's and the teacher's score
• Grades 9-12; music technology or music production classes
• The deck uses the free Oswald and Source Sans 3 fonts (built into Google Slides); install them for PowerPoint or Keynote, or present the PDF copy, which shows the intended look
• Every student page prints well in black and white

Logic Pro is a product of Apple Inc., named only to describe compatibility. This resource is not affiliated with Apple.

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
