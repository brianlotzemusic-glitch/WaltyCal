The one thing to do on Class 1

Ask each student to name where their music could be used (my first tip) and to write three words on the Creative brief before they leave. If a word is only what they like, ask: how would you play that word to someone who had never heard it?

Classes 2–4

Working days; Class 4 has one slide. Circulate with one question: which of your three words is this layer for?

Royalty FreeWorked example · an invented piece
EXAMPLE
Teacher

An invented piece, “Open Doors”, described on paper, for a one-minute tour of a renovated school library (an invented video): no audio is included. Students’ projects will differ. 30 bars at 120 BPM, three words, four sections, seven layers (page 1 of 2).

1 · A weak plan

I will make a happy song with a nice beat.

It names no words and nothing says why any choice was made.

2 · A clear plan

My video is a one-minute tour of a renovated school library (an invented video). My three words are Bright, Steady and Uplifting. Bright: bells and plucked keys in a major key, with the highs lifted a little. Steady: 120 BPM that never changes, a kick on every beat and one chord for every two bars. Uplifting: a bell melody that climbs, a new layer every few bars, and an ending that lands on the home chord. It is 30 bars, 60 seconds, in four sections: intro, build, peak and resolve. Two layers are Apple Loops that I cut and rearrange.
