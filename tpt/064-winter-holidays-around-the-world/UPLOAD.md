## 2. Description

```
Bring music and culture together with 5 short readings and 5 listening response pages for grades 3-6. Students read about Christmas, Hanukkah, Kwanzaa, Las Posadas and Lunar New Year, listen to music you choose from each tradition, and describe what they hear: instruments, tempo, dynamics, mood and beat feel. The readings are 80 to 120 words each and keep to basic, widely documented facts. Each one comes with 3 reading questions and an answer key.

No audio is included and no recordings are named. You choose any music you have the right to use, such as public domain or traditional music, a licensed recording or a live performance, and write it in the teacher-chosen example box. The teacher notes frame the pages as learning about cultures, not practicing a religion, invite student voices without assumptions about any family, and note that Lunar New Year falls in late January or February, so you can teach this pack in December, January or February.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (21 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover (1 page)
• Teacher notes: respectful framing, how to choose music you have the right to use, the calendar, facts at a glance, pacing and support (2 pages)
• How to listen guide with word choices for the five music elements (1 page)
• Vocabulary page with 12 words (1 page)
• 5 short readings, one each for Christmas, Hanukkah, Kwanzaa, Las Posadas and Lunar New Year, with 3 reading questions each (5 pages)
• 5 listening response pages with 6 prompts each: instruments, tempo, dynamics, mood, beat feel and a connection to the reading (5 pages)
• Compare and contrast page with a table and a Venn diagram (1 page)
• Five-celebration chart (1 page)
• Rubric with 5 categories, 20 points (1 page)
• Reflection page (1 page)
• Reading answer key (1 page)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)
Total: 21 pages.

HOW TO USE
For each celebration, students read the passage and answer the reading questions, listen once, listen again and fill in the response page, then share. One celebration takes about 25 minutes. Finish with the compare and contrast pages and the reflection. Grades 3-4 can listen together and circle choices; grades 5-6 can write full sentences and use the vocabulary.

• Grades 3-6, no prep and no audio to download
• Five music elements on every response page
• Prints well in black and white; one accent color

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
