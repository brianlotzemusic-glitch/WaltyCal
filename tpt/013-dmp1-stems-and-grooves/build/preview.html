Stems & GroovesThe four transitions
STUDENT
Reference

A transition is the moment between two sections where the drums signal that a change is coming. Each example shows one measure of the groove, then the transition measure (green) right before the new section. Each grid column is a sixteenth note; grey columns are the beats.

1 · Drum filla rhythmic variation in the last 1–2 measures that signals a change is coming.

Use it: the classic: works almost anywhere, especially into a bigger section.

2 · Breakdownstrip to one element (kick only, hi-hat only) before the new section hits.

Use it: before a section that should hit harder, or as the music thins out.

3 · Cutcomplete silence for 1–2 beats before the next section drops.

Use it: the most powerful and the easiest to do badly; a cut before a quiet verse.

4 · Buildadd elements progressively in the last measure to increase energy into the change.

Use it: a build before a big chorus.

Requirements ·

PART 1 — FOUNDATION
Choose one stem track from the Foundation folder on your class page
Listen to the full track and identify at least two distinct sections (A and B) before building anything
Write an A pattern: a drum groove that fits the feel of one section type
Write a B pattern: a drum groove that clearly contrasts with A in feel, energy, or rhythm
Place patterns correctly so they follow the section changes in the track
Minimum 3 layers per pattern (kick, snare, hi-hat at minimum)
All track volumes adjusted so nothing clips and every element is audible
Project named correctly: “Your Name - Foundation”
Exported as MP3 and .zip (compressed .logicx file) and submitted via your class page before presenting
PART 2 — FULL SONG
Using a new Full Song stem from your class page, identify every section change in the track
At each section change, add a deliberate transition using one of the four options: drum fill, breakdown, cut, or build
Every section change must have a transition… no pattern can simply loop through a boundary without acknowledgment
At least two different transition types must be used across the full track
Transitions should feel appropriate to the music… a cut before a quiet verse, a build before a big chorus
All track volumes and panning adjusted… mix serves the stem, not the other way around
Project named correctly: “Your Name - Full Song”
Exported as MP3 and .zip (compressed .logicx file) and submitted via your class page before presenting
