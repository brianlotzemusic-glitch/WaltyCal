"Differ in 11 of 16 cells" is exact (hat 4, kick 3, open hat 4). The four transition grids match their descriptions and the key (fill: groove to beat 2, snare sixteenths, high then low toms; breakdown: kick on every beat; cut: 2 beats of silence; build: 1, 2, 3, 4 sounds on beats 1-4). Quiz answers verified from the grids: fill, build (1, 1, 2, 3), cut (1 beat of silence), breakdown (hi-hat only).
- Energy table and transitions: energy = instrument count, matches the recipe (1, 2, 4, 2, 4, 2, 4, 1). "Every build leads into more energy (1 to 2, 2 to 4) and the cut leads into less (4 to 2)": true.

Fix: "Every chord is a white-key chord from the C major card in Unit 3 (I, iii, IV, V and vi)". Also note "The Day 1 post's 'half the kicks'" in the bridge row is fine.
- Optional: "a cut before a quiet verse" (owner's rule) vs the example's cut before the bridge: the page says "Here ... the cut leads into less", which covers it.
