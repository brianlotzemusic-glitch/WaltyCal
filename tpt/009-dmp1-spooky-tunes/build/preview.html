Requirements ·

Reverb bus set up correctly in Logic: aux channel, Bus 1, Space Designer at 100% wet
Delay bus set up correctly in Logic: aux channel, Bus 2, Tape Delay or Echo at 100% wet
At least 3 tracks sending to the reverb bus
At least 1 track sending to the delay bus
Two different minor keys: key 1 for measures 1–16, key 2 for measures 17–32 (atonal sound effects can sit on top)
Minimum 5 distinct sound sources (a combination of the Logic library, your own recording, and a free download)
At least 1 original recording made with a microphone
At least 1 sound downloaded from a free sound-effects site (such as pixabay.com) and imported into Logic
Music composed to the full length of the video: 32 measures, about 1 minute
No empty silence longer than 5 seconds unless it is a deliberate compositional choice
Project named: Your Name – Spooky Tunes
Exported as MP3 and .zip (compressed .logicx file) and submitted to your class page before presenting
A short written reflection: two specific techniques you used to create atmosphere, and why you chose them

Sound sources ·

You have three places to find sounds for this project. Use all three.

Logic Pro libraryOpen the Loop Browser (O) or the Library. Search by keyword: try “dark”, “tension”, “drone”, “cinematic”, “wind”, “impact”. You are not limited to loops: individual one-shots and samples work well for SFX layers.
Your own recordingRecord your own sounds in the room. Scratch a surface. Close a door slowly. Tap something hollow. Crumple paper. Hum. Any recorded sound can become something unsettling with enough processing. Drag the recording into Logic and apply effects.
A free downloadGo to a free sound-effects site your teacher approves (for example pixabay.com › Sound Effects) and search for atmospheric or horror sounds. Download as MP3, then drag the file from your Downloads folder into the Tracks area to create a new audio track. Check the site’s license.

HOW TO BUS
2 of 4 · Reverb

Part 1 · Set up your reverb bus ·

Start from the track you want to add reverb to, not from a blank aux channel. Logic creates the bus automatically when you make the send.

  1. 1
    Open the channel strip for your trackIn the Mixer (X) or the inspector on the left, find the channel strip for the track you want to add reverb to. Look for the Sends section: it sits below the insert plugin slots.
    If you cannot see Sends, widen the inspector or the Mixer so the Sends slots show.
  2. 2
    Click an empty Send slot and choose a busIn the Sends section, click one of the empty slots. A menu appears. Go to Bus and choose Bus 1 (any bus that shows no name next to it is empty).
    Watch what happens: Logic automatically creates an Aux channel strip routed to that bus. You do not need to create it manually.
  3. 3
    Raise the Send levelA small knob now appears in the send slot. At its lowest, nothing is going to the bus. Click and drag it upward. Start around –15 dB.
    This is the most critical step. A send knob at its lowest sends nothing. Nothing will happen until you raise it.
  4. 4
    Add Space Designer to the Aux channelFind the Aux channel Logic just created in the Mixer. Click its first empty Insert slot and choose Reverb › Space Designer. For Spooky Tunes, try a large hall, cathedral or other large-space preset.
    The Aux channel may appear at the far right of the Mixer. Scroll if you cannot find it.
  5. 5
    Set Space Designer to 100% wetInside Space Designer, set the Dry signal to 0% and the Wet signal to 100%. The bus outputs only the reverb. The dry signal always comes from the original track.
    This is the most common mistake. If the plugin is partially dry, you hear the sound doubled. Always 100% wet on a bus plugin.
  6. 6
    Name the Aux channelDouble-click the name at the bottom of the Aux channel strip and type REVERB BUS.
    You only set this up once. Every other track that needs reverb just needs a send pointing to the same bus number.
  7. 7
    Send more tracks to the same busSelect another track. Click an empty Send slot. Choose the same bus number as before. Raise the send level. That track now shares the reverb.
    Do not pick a new bus number for each track. All reverb sends point to the same bus.
