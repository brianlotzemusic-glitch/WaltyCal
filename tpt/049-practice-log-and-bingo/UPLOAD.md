Get students practicing at home with a music practice log and a Practice Bingo game. This printable pack has 4 weekly practice logs, a 30-day practice tracker, 4 different bingo boards, a challenge list, a parent note and teacher notes. It works for band, orchestra, choir, piano and guitar students in grades 4 to 8, and the wording is the same for every instrument and for voice.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (15 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover (1 page)
• Teacher notes: a four-week plan, grading ideas and differentiation for grades 4-5 and grades 6-8 (1 page)
• Parent note: a friendly letter template with blanks and a reply slip (1 page)
• How to win: rows, columns, diagonals and blackout, with pictures (1 page)
• Weekly practice logs: minutes for each day, what I practiced, a 1 to 5 self-rating, a parent or guardian initials column, a goals line and a reflection (4 pages, one for each of 4 weeks)
• 30-day practice tracker: a calendar-style grid with weekly totals, a 30-day total and streak rewards (1 page)
• Challenge list and caller sheet: all 48 challenges, numbered (1 page)
• Practice Bingo boards: 4 different 5 x 5 boards, each with 24 challenges and a FREE center (4 pages)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)
Total: 15 pages.

HOW TO USE
Hand out one log and one bingo board each week for four weeks, and start the 30-day tracker on day 1. Students write down their minutes, mark the bingo squares they finish and ask a parent or guardian to initial the log. Challenges include "play your slowest section 3 times", "practice with a metronome" and "teach someone a rhythm". Each of the 48 challenges appears on 2 of the 4 boards. Good for homework, practice charts, a class bingo game and parent communication.
