## 2. Description

```
Make music dynamics and tempo easy to see with 12 printable anchor chart posters, a one-page student reference sheet and a 10-question quiz with an answer key. Each poster has a large heading, a short definition, a simple picture and the symbol where there is one. It works for general music, band, orchestra and choir in grades 2 to 8, and nothing in it is tied to a season or a holiday.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (19 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover (1 page)
• Teacher notes: how to display the posters, a rotating word wall, games, and differentiation for grades 2-4 and grades 5-8 (1 page)
• 12 printable posters, one per page (12 pages): pianissimo (pp), piano (p), mezzo piano (mp), mezzo forte (mf), forte (f), fortissimo (ff), crescendo (cresc.), decrescendo and diminuendo (decresc., dim.), largo and adagio, andante and moderato, allegro and presto, accelerando (accel.) and ritardando (rit.)
• Student reference sheet with all terms, symbols and meanings (1 page)
• Quiz with 10 questions worth 20 points: matching, multiple choice, order the dynamics from softest to loudest, order the tempos from slowest to fastest (2 pages)
• Quiz answer key (1 page)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)
Total: 19 pages.

HOW TO USE
Hang the posters as a word wall or rotate two or three at a time, hand out the reference sheet, and finish with the quiz. The tempo posters give about BPM ranges (beats per minute), such as largo about 40-60 and presto about 168-200, and say that books and teachers use slightly different numbers.

• Grades 2–8 (differentiation for grades 2-4 and grades 5-8 in the teacher notes)
• Prints well in black and white; one accent color
• No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
