## 2. Description

```
Winter Bucket Drumming gives your class 6 original four-bar grooves for bucket drums, each written as a play-along chart on a one-line rhythm staff. Every groove has three parts that layer: Part 1 Bass (low and steady), Part 2 Groove (the main pattern) and Part 3 Sparkle (rim clicks and rests). That is 18 parts in all, and every part has the same meter (4/4) and the same four bars, so the beats line up when you stack them. Every note has a hand letter (R or L) under it and a say-it line of rhythm syllables (ta, ti-ti, shh, ta-a), so students can say it, pat it and then play it. Grooves 1 to 4 use quarter notes, quarter rests, half notes and eighth notes, and Grooves 5 and 6 add sixteenth notes. The grooves are original, so no tune is used. For music classes in grades 3 to 8.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (16 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover (1 page)
• Teacher notes with pacing, differentiation and assessment (1 page)
• Safety and setup (1 page)
• Notation key (two pages) (2 pages)
• Layering activity (1 page)
• Sample score (1 page)
• Groove charts, one page per groove (6 grooves, 18 parts) (6 pages)
• Ensemble planner (1 page)
• Rubric and score sheet (1 page)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)
Total: 16 pages, 6 grooves, 18 parts.

HOW TO USE
Start with the safety and setup page and the notation key, then teach one groove at a time. Students say and pat each part, then build the groove in ensembles of 3 or 4: Bass starts, Groove joins, Sparkle joins. The sample score shows the three parts stacked on one page so the bars line up. The pacing plan runs over 4 sessions, and the teacher notes give differentiation for grades 3-5 and grades 6-8. The ensemble planner and the rubric (total parts played correctly out of 18, plus a 12-point skills rubric) cover assessment.

• Grades 3–8 (all 6 grooves, from quarter and eighth notes to sixteenth notes)
• Safety and care notes: clean plastic buckets, sticks or hands, protecting hearing
• Notation key that defines every symbol used: notes, rests, accents, rim clicks, hand letters
• Prints well in black and white

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
