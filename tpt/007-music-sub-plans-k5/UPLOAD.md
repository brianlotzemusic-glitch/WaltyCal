## 2. Description

```
Five no-prep, no-tech music sub plans for grades K–5 that any substitute can teach, even with no music background. Each day has a step-by-step plan with timed steps and words to say, a closing game, and two worksheets: one for grades K–2 and one for grades 3–5. Every rhythm has its syllables (ta, ti-ti, shh, ta-a) printed under the notes, so the sub can lead echo clapping without reading music.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (25 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover and teacher notes with a page guide and sub-tub setup steps (2 pages)
• Welcome sheet to fill in for your sub: classes, procedures, where things are (1 page)
• Days at a glance plus a game bank of 7 no-prep music games (1 page)
• Big rhythm chart: 8 echo lines with syllables under every note (1 page)
• 5 sub plans, one per day (5 pages)
• 10 worksheets: a K–2 and a 3–5 sheet for each day (10 pages)
• Early finishers page for any grade (1 page)
• Answer keys for Days 2, 3 and 5 (2 pages)
• Sub report form for notes back to you (1 page)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)

THE FIVE DAYS
• Day 1, Sound Detectives: listening log. K–2 draw and circle loud/soft, high/low, long/short; 3–5 describe dynamics, pitch, length and timbre
• Day 2, Rhythm Readers: steady beat and rhythm reading. K–2 count the sounds and match word rhythms; 3–5 rhythm math, spot the bars that are not 4 beats, write the syllables
• Day 3, Loud & Soft, Fast & Slow: dynamics and tempo. K–2 forte and piano, fast or slow; 3–5 match pp to ff and crescendo/decrescendo, put tempo words in order
• Day 4, Rhythm Composers: K–2 build 4-beat rhythms from words; 3–5 compose 4 bars in 4/4 with a composer checklist, then a class concert
• Day 5, Music Word Games: K–2 music opposites and tracing; 3–5 music word search and word work

WHY IT WORKS FOR SUBS
• No tech, no instruments, no music reading needed: voices, hands, pencils and a few everyday classroom objects
• Each day is one 30–45 minute class, and the days work in any order
• Classic classroom games with original directions: Echo Me, Poison Rhythm, Hot and Cold Dynamics, Silent Conductor, Rhythm Telephone, Freeze Beat, Copy My Sound

ACCURATE BY DESIGN
Every rhythm is engraved with a professional music font and was checked by computer: each bar holds 4 beats (except the bars on Day 2 that students must spot as wrong), and every answer key comes from the same checked data.

• Grades K–5, with separate K–2 and 3–5 worksheets every day
• Quarter notes, eighth-note pairs, quarter rests, half notes and whole notes in 4/4
• Prints well in black and white
• All lessons, chants, games, words and art are original. No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters

Designed with the help of digital and AI tools, and checked by hand.
```
