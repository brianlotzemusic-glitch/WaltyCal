GLYPHS
4 listening glyphs

A listening glyph is a quick-response picture. Draw it, stamp it or circle it to show what you noticed. The glyphs also appear on page 6, page 7, page 8, page 10, page 11, page 13, page 14 and page 15.

ContourMelody contour

It means: The melody moves: it goes up, goes down, or stays level.

Stamp it when you notice the tune rising, falling or leaping.

Use with the melody shape page (page 10). Add a word: up, down, level, steps or leaps.

PulseRhythm and beat

It means: The steady beat, and the pattern of long and short sounds on top of it.

Stamp it when you can tap the beat or hear a rhythm pattern.

Use with the beat and meter page (page 11). Add a word: steady, speeding up or slowing down.

DynamicsLoud and soft

It means: How loud or soft the music is, and when it gets louder or softer.

Stamp it when the volume changes or stays strong.

Use with the tempo and dynamics page (page 8). Add a letter or word: p, mp, mf, f, crescendo or decrescendo.

TimbreInstrument color

It means: The color of a sound: what makes a flute sound different from a drum.

Stamp it when you notice an instrument or voice and how it sounds.

Use with the instruments page (page 7). Add the instrument or a color word, such as bright or warm.

WordSay itWhat it means
Beat and rhythm6 words
beat The steady pulse in music, like a heartbeat you can tap.
rhythm The pattern of long and short sounds and silences over the beat.
meter How the beats are grouped into strong and weak, usually in sets of 2, 3 or 4.
measure One group of beats between two bar lines. It is also called a bar.
accent A note played or sung with extra stress so it stands out.
rest A silence that lasts for a certain number of beats.

Melody and pitch6 words
pitch How high or low a sound is.
melody The tune: a line of pitches you could hum or sing.
contour The shape of a melody as it moves up, down or stays level.
step A move from one note to the very next note, up or down.
leap A jump from one note to a note that is farther away.
phrase A musical sentence: an idea in the melody with a beginning and an ending.
