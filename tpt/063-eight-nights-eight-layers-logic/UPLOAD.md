## 2. Description

```
Layered arrangement project for high school music technology: students build 32 bars of music in Logic Pro in eight sections of 4 bars, and every section adds one new track, so the arrangement grows from one layer to eight: pad or drone, bass, kick and clap, hi-hat or shaker, chord stabs, arpeggio, counter-melody and lead. The structure is inspired by the eight nights of Hanukkah (Chanukah), when one more candle is added each night; it is a music-technology project, not a religious activity, and it suits any class (students may choose a different eight-step theme). This Eight Nights, Eight Layers project takes 3 class periods (the pacing guide also shows two and four) and is an original Hudson Beat project; the steps are written for Logic Pro, while the ideas (layers, sections, tempo math) carry over to GarageBand.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (ZIP: 23-page PDF = 1 cover + 9 teacher + 12 student + 1 terms page, plus 14-slide intro deck as .pptx and PDF)
• Teacher pages: how to use, project overview with a short, respectful note on the festival that inspired the structure, setup, a 3-class pacing guide with the deck slides for each class, two pages of teacher notes (struggles, variations, resources, grading), a Logic quick reference and a two-page worked example (an invented arrangement at 100 BPM: 32 bars = 76.8 seconds, with the eight entrance times and a map) (9 pages)
• Student pages: the layer map (a staircase of 8 layers by 8 sections), eight layer cards with a role, tools to try and a pattern idea, a tempo table (seconds per beat, bar and section, the length of 32 bars and the start time of every section at 80, 90, 100, 110, 120 and 128 BPM), an arrangement plan, a mix checklist, the 3-page project sheet (overview, goals, vocabulary, requirements, steps, tips, submission checklist), the /25 rubric, a vocabulary page, a feedback planner and a reflection page (12 pages)
• Teacher-presented intro deck, 14 slides with speaker notes on every slide: a think-and-share hook, what the project is, the layer map, the inspiration, seconds and bars, the eight layers, building it in Logic, a worked-example map, what to turn in and the rubric

THE THREE CLASS PERIODS
• Class 1: hook; the layer map and the eight layers; choose a key, chords and a tempo; plan the layers
• Class 2: set up Logic with a marker at every section; build the first six layers, one per section
• Class 3: the counter-melody and the lead; mix, bounce, present and reflect

WHAT STUDENTS LEARN
• Layering: what a pad, a bass, drums, stabs, an arpeggio, a counter-melody and a lead are each for
• Timing math: seconds per beat = 60 ÷ BPM, seconds per bar in 4/4 = 240 ÷ BPM, and the length of 32 bars = 32 × 240 ÷ BPM (at 100 BPM, 76.8 seconds)
• Building in Logic Pro: markers for sections, repeating a region, a pad or lead in Alchemy or Retro Synth, Drum Machine Designer, Quick Sampler, and Session Players, Step Sequencer or Arpeggiator if your version has them; automation, mixing and bounce
• Mixing so that every new layer can be heard, and explaining their choices in a reflection

GOOD TO KNOW
• There is no audio in this resource and no student work. No melody, song or lyrics are printed or notated; students make every sound themselves in Logic Pro. The lead can be a melody the student writes or any melody they have the right to use
• Requires Logic Pro on a Mac. A MIDI keyboard is not needed; nobody has to read music. Where a control may differ between versions, the pages say "if your version has it"
• Rubric: 5 categories scored 1-5, /25; the final grade is the average of the student's and the teacher's score
• Grades 9-12; music technology or music production classes
• The deck uses the free Oswald and Source Sans 3 fonts (built into Google Slides); install them for PowerPoint or Keynote, or present the PDF copy, which shows the intended look
• Every student page prints well in black and white

Logic Pro is a product of Apple Inc., named only to describe compatibility. This resource is not affiliated with Apple.

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
