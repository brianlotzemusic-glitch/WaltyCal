# TpT music market research and factory game plan (23 Nov 2026)

Owner request: "Check out a bunch of the top rated and highest sold products on TPT. Synthesize what they do with copy, pictures,
pricing and ideas. Offer a game plan to adjust the factory to maximize profits."

## What was studied, and how far to trust it
- 288 products: the Performing Arts > Music category sorted by number of ratings (the owner's suggested URL, `order=Rating-Count`).
- About 2,000 more products from 35 music searches (relevance and rating order), including a music-technology sweep (Logic Pro,
  GarageBand, BandLab, Soundtrap, beat making, songwriting).
- 60 product pages read in full (description, price, release date, review dates, thumbnails) and 40+ covers looked at.
- Our own public store page (60 listings at the time).
- Limits. TpT shows ratings counts, not sales. Review counts build up over years, so the all-time list is dominated by listings
  10-14 years old. "Revenue proxy" below means price x review count: a rough guide, not money. Descriptions of how TpT ranks search
  results could not be verified (the web search found only seller-coach opinion), so none is claimed here.
- Data: `tpt/_research/top-music-by-rating-count-2026-11-23.json` (top 120). Covers: `tpt/_research/market-sheet*.jpg`.

## 1. Where the money is
| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Bundles and mega packs carry the dollars | Bundles, mega packs and programs are 31% of the top 288 products and 28% of reviews but ~69% of the revenue proxy. Products priced $25+ are 18% of the list and ~59% of the proxy. |
| Singles sit at $3-8 | 59% of the top 288 cost under $8; median price $6.50; median rating 4.9 (a 4.8+ rating is table stakes). The densest bands are $4-4.99 (16%), $6-7.99 (15%), $5-5.99 (14%). |
| Posters, listening guides, songs and games out-review worksheets | Average reviews per product: songs/lyrics 848, posters/bulletin boards 484, listening/composer 347, movement/drumming 307, games 284, sub plans 260, worksheets/theory 231. |
| Breadth wins: the top sellers carry 20-31 music products each | Music with Sara Bibee 31 (13,335 reviews), The Bulletin Board Lady 27, Lindsay Jervis 20, Pitch Publications 20, Cori Bloom 11. We already have 60+ listings; our gap is reviews and bundles, not catalogue size. |
| Series and seasonal repeats are the engine | Sara Bibee's "No Prep Mega Pack of Music Worksheets" exists for Autumn, Christmas, Spring and Summer at $10 each with 450-590 reviews each. Enharmonic Education's Music Bingo (2023, the only newer music winner) has a version per season and holiday at $5.25 (554, 286, 96, 48... reviews) plus a $168 "32 games" mega bundle (168 reviews). |
| The leaderboard is old; new entrants are slow | Of the top 288, 276 have the oldest listing numbers (under 5 million). Median review count of music listings newer than ~15M is 0-2. Our zero public reviews so far (the store is about five weeks old) is normal; reviews, followers and bundles are what compound. |
| Evergreen dominates | Only ~10% of top titles are seasonal, but they out-review non-seasonal ones (median 286 vs 241). Evergreen = steady; seasonal = spikes. |

## 2. What the winners do
**Titles (median 56 characters, max 80).** Plain search words first (the thing and the format: "Christmas Music Bingo Game", "Elementary Music
Sub Plans for the Non-Music Sub"), then use-case words buyers type ("Fun Friday", "Whole Class Reward", "Brain Break", "Indoor
Recess", "No Prep", "Print and Go"), then grade band or season. No cute names. 'No prep' titles: median 315 reviews vs 241 without.

**Descriptions (median 2,200 characters, up to the 3,500 TpT shows).** Opening line names the teacher's problem and the payoff ("Is it
taking too much time to write sub plans?"). Then WHAT'S INCLUDED (50% of the top 60), "students love it" language (78%), a bundle and
save cross-link (48%), a feedback request (63%: "leave feedback, earn TpT credits"), a follow-my-store link (37%), digital/Google
Slides versions (38%), and sub-friendly framing (45%). Reviewers praise "students loved / were engaged" (39%) and "easy, no prep,
ready" (25%). None cites standards much (7%).

**Pictures.** 51 of 60 top listings use exactly four thumbnails, each with a job: (1) cover: giant bold title, bright colour, a
friendly character or hero image, one or two callouts ("NO PREP PRINT AND GO", "200+ PAGES!", "BUNDLE", "with bonus PLAYLIST!"), a
page mockup in a corner, a series brand bar; (2) the real page with a callout ("50 cards in colour + 50 in B&W"); (3) how it works
("Print. Shuffle. Play!"); (4) teacher notes, what's included, or extras. The top covers are loud and colourful, not minimal. Ours
(`tpt/_research/market-sheetC.jpg`, bottom row) are clean and professional but much quieter at thumbnail size, with no callouts
or page mockup. The music-tech competition (GarageBand/BandLab/Soundtrap kits) has weak covers: ours are already better there.

**Pricing.** Singles $3-7 (median $6.50); units/themes $10-15; bundles $24-60; programs $70-170. Odd prices ($5.25, $23.96, $71.50)
appear on high performers. Bundles run ~30-35% under the sum.

**Ideas that keep selling (music, ranked by review count):** educational songs and lyric posters; classroom-management songs; elements-of-music
and "what to listen for" posters/bulletin boards; sub plans for non-music subs (the sub-tub formula); listening journals and
glyphs for classical works (Peter and the Wolf 640, John Williams 622, Carnival of the Animals 510, Nutcracker 506, The Planets 504);
scarf and ribbon movement cards; bucket drumming; recorder fingering charts; no-prep seasonal worksheet packs; Jeopardy-style review
games in PowerPoint/Slides (811 reviews, $5); Black History Month musician boards (509); back-to-school ice breakers; yearly
planners; whole-class reward games (bingo).

## 3. Music technology (our Logic niche)
- 477 results across the tech searches; 158 with DAW/production words in the title. Top reviews: GarageBand unit 43 ($10), BandLab
  15 ($27), Soundtrap 12. Nothing in the niche has more than 50 reviews: low ceiling, low competition. We hold 15 of the 158
  (second to Vinci eLearn, 20). Price median about $7; our $9-12 kits and $49 bundle are at the top end, which looks fine because
  our covers and depth are stronger. Logic Pro is Mac-only; the competition mostly uses GarageBand, BandLab and Soundtrap
  (iPad and Chromebook schools). A web-based variant of a winning kit is untested demand.

## 4. What our factory is doing differently
- 72 items live. **Four of the five bundles in `tpt/QUEUE.md` already meet their build condition and have never been built**
  (DMP2 Projects Bundle: 030 is live; Holiday Logic Pro Pack Vol. 1: 8 holiday kits live; Winter and Holiday Music Mega Bundle: 25
  winter items live; Color by Note Mega Bundle: 7 sets live). The shifts keep taking single products. This is the biggest gap.
- No listing carries a bundle cross-sell, a feedback ask or a follow ask in the description.
- Covers have no callouts or mockups; titles are good but rarely carry use-case words.
- About 29% of output is Logic kits (the niche with the smallest proven demand), 1 a day. Sales data may justify it; none was available.
- Product ideas we make that the data supports: sub plans, bingo/games, seasonal worksheets, bucket drumming, listening units, boards.
  Ideas the data supports that we have not made: listening journals for public-domain classics, poster/bulletin-board sets (elements of
  music, what to listen for, instrument families is queued), composer of the month, scarf/ribbon movement cards, Jeopardy-style
  PowerPoint/Slides games, yearly planners, Music In Our Schools Month (March) and Black History Month sets.

## 5. Game plan
**Phase 0, this week (no new product ideas needed)**
1. Build the four unlocked bundles, in this order: Winter and Holiday Music Mega Bundle (live by 1 Dec), Holiday Logic Pro Pack Vol. 1,
   Color by Note Mega Bundle (as a **Growing Bundle**: a price that rises as each new set is added, the pattern in the top five),
   DMP2 Projects Bundle. About 30-35% off. Cover: collage, "SAVE 30%", item count. Each included listing then shows "Also included in".
2. Put three standard lines in every new description and the template: bundle and save link, "leave feedback to earn TpT credits",
   follow-the-store. Add a closing "Please rate this" page to every new PDF (reviews are what compound).
3. Get our own sales numbers: top 10 by earnings and by views for the last 30 days (owner screenshot, or a read-only Mac job that exports
   the Seller Dashboard). Every decision below gets sharper with it.

**Phase 1, next 2-4 weeks**
4. Cover 2.0 for all new builds: keep the Hudson Beat type identity and house line art, but fill the square (bigger hero from the
   art session), one accent colour block, two callouts (NO PREP, 3 LEVELS, 27 PAGES, ANSWER KEYS), a page mockup inset, a series
   bar. Thumbnails 2-4: real page with a count callout; "how it works" in three steps; teacher notes and what's included.
5. A/B test on 6 live listings (new covers through the replacement pipeline, which now works), compare views and sales in 3 weeks.
6. Titles: primary search phrase, then use-case words (Fun Friday, Whole Class Reward, Brain Break, Sub Plans, No Prep, Print and Go),
   then grade band; stay within 80 characters.
7. Prices: singles $4.50-6.50 for 20+ page packs (we sit at $3.50-5.50 against a $6.50 median); units $8-12; bundles per
   rule above. Raise 5 best sellers by $1 once the dashboard shows conversion.

**Phase 2, the weekly mix (about 21 builds)**
- 40% evergreen formats the data proves: listening journals for public-domain works (Carnival of the Animals 1886, The Planets 1916,
  Peer Gynt 1875, Pictures at an Exhibition 1874, William Tell 1829, Danse Macabre 1874, Four Seasons 1725); poster and bulletin-board sets;
  no-tech sub plans in a numbered Sub Tub series; bingo/games series; no-prep worksheet packs; Jeopardy-style Slides and PowerPoint games.
- 30% seasonal, shipped early: Black History Month (live by 15 Jan), Valentine's (20 Jan), Music In Our Schools Month (15 Feb), St.
  Patrick's (1 Mar), spring, end of year, back to school (15 Jul), Halloween (1 Sep). Each season gets a series, a bundle, and a free sampler.
- 15% bundles and growing-bundle updates; 15% Logic kits (2-3 a week) unless the dashboard shows they convert better than classroom items;
  then test one BandLab/Soundtrap/GarageBand variant of the best kit.

**Phase 3, keep the flywheel**
- New release every day matters (a seller-coach source cites a 2025 TpT seller survey naming consistent new releases among top factors; unverified).
- One free sampler per season to build followers; followers are notified of new products.
- Re-check this research monthly: re-run the harvest and compare ranks.

## Do not copy
Popular-song lists, characters, brand names (Enharmonic's bingo uses pop songs); AI art for notation, text or keys; anything that
depends on recordings we do not own.
