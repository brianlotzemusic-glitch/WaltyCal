## 2. Description

```
Need sub plans a substitute can teach without any music background, any technology, or any prep from you? These eight February music days are ready to print and hand over. Each day has a step-by-step plan with timed steps and words to say, plus two student sheets: one for grades K–2 and one for grades 3–5. The theme is February: short, respectful facts about five Black musicians for Black History Month (Scott Joplin, Duke Ellington, Ella Fitzgerald, Louis Armstrong and Florence Price), the heartbeat as a steady beat for Valentine's Day, the drums, cymbals and gong of the Lunar New Year lion dance, and a little Groundhog Day fun.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (48 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover and teacher notes with the February note, setup steps and a page guide (2 pages)
• Sub quick start, welcome sheet, days at a glance, quick games, word-rhythm chart, February facts and a class sign (7 pages)
• 8 sub plans, one per day, each 30–45 minutes (8 pages)
• 16 student sheets: a K–2 and a 3–5 sheet for each day (16 pages)
• Day extras: movement picture cards, chant word cards, a chant book page and a read-aloud quiz (4 pages)
• Early finishers (2 pages)
• Exit tickets (1 page)
• Answer keys for Days 1, 2, 5, 6, 7 and 8 (3 pages)
• Sub report form, folder labels, class star awards, terms of use and a thank-you page (5 pages)

THE EIGHT DAYS
• Day 1, Musician Spotlight Reader: K–2 hear a short reading about five Black musicians and circle answers; 3–5 read a passage of about 200 words and answer five questions
• Day 2, Name Beats: clap the syllables of musicians’ names and February words; K–2 color a circle for each clap, 3–5 sort words by syllables and build a four-beat chant
• Day 3, Heart Drum Design Lab: design a heart drum; K–2 draw it, name it and finish two sentence starters, 3–5 label three parts and write two sentences
• Day 4, Heartbeat Freeze: move to a tapped beat and freeze, with 8 picture cards; K–2 circle how they moved, 3–5 reflect and design a freeze game
• Day 5, Lantern Word Puzzles: February word puzzles; K–2 match words to pictures and trace, 3–5 word search plus a decode-the-message puzzle
• Day 6, Music Job Spotlight: five music jobs, from composer to piano tuner; K–2 draw a job, 3–5 match jobs and write a job ad or interview questions
• Day 7, Lantern Chant Book: build four-beat chants from heart, drum and lantern word cards, mark the syllables, share with a partner, and keep a chant book page
• Day 8, February Bingo Showdown: bingo (12 squares for K–2, 16 for 3–5), a read-aloud quiz and Pass the Pencil, with a reflection

WHY IT WORKS FOR SUBS
• No tech: nothing needs a computer, projector, speaker or video. Paper, pencils, crayons, hands and voices only
• No music skills: rhythm is taught through speech rhythm, clapping the syllables of words, with the claps printed as dots. The sub never reads music or sings
• No prep: print, put each day in a folder, and go. Each day works in any order for a mixed K–5 class
• Facts are short, sourced and secular, so the days work in public schools and for students of every background

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
