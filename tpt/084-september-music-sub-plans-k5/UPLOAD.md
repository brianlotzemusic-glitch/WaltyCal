## 2. Description

```
Need sub plans a substitute can teach without any music background, any technology, or any prep from you? These eight September music days are ready to print and hand over. Each day has a step-by-step plan with timed steps and words to say, plus two student sheets: one for grades K–2 and one for grades 3–5. The theme is September: Hispanic Heritage Month (short, true facts and five instruments, the guitar, maracas, güiro, marimba and cajón, each tied to the place or region that sources give, with a teacher note on teaching it with care), Labor Day and music at work, and the start of fall with back to school.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (48 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover and teacher notes with the September note, a Heritage Month teaching note, setup steps and a page guide (2 pages)
• Sub quick start, welcome sheet, days at a glance, quick games, word-rhythm chart, September facts and a class sign (7 pages)
• 8 sub plans, one per day, each 30–45 minutes (8 pages)
• 16 student sheets: a K–2 and a 3–5 sheet for each day (16 pages)
• Day extras: movement picture cards, chant word cards, a chant book page and a read-aloud quiz (4 pages)
• Early finishers (2 pages)
• Exit tickets (1 page)
• Answer keys for Days 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8; Day 3 is open-ended and its entry says what to look for (3 pages)
• Sub report form, folder labels, class star awards, terms of use and a thank-you page (5 pages)

THE EIGHT DAYS
• Day 1, September Snapshots: K–2 hear a short reading about Hispanic Heritage Month, Labor Day and the start of fall and circle answers; 3–5 read a passage of about 200 words and answer five questions
• Day 2, Say It and Clap It: clap the syllables of September words and a short original four-line poem; K–2 color a circle for each clap, 3–5 sort words by syllables and build a four-beat chant
• Day 3, Instrument Sketchbook: meet the guitar, maracas, güiro, marimba and cajón, then draw one or invent your own and label it (paper only); K–2 draw, circle how its sound is made and label two parts, 3–5 label three parts and write two sentences
• Day 4, Strum, Shake and Freeze: move and freeze to a tapped beat, try call and response, with 8 picture cards (guitar, maracas, güiro, marimba, cajón, leaf, hammer, apple); K–2 circle how they moved, 3–5 reflect and design a freeze game
• Day 5, September Puzzle Pack: instrument, Heritage Month and fall word puzzles; K–2 match words to pictures and trace, 3–5 word search plus a decode-the-message puzzle
• Day 6, Music at Work: Labor Day and music at work: five music jobs, from conductor to radio host; K–2 draw a job, 3–5 match jobs and write a job ad or interview questions
• Day 7, My Fall Chant Book: build four-beat chants from September word cards, mark the syllables, share with a partner, and keep a chant book page
• Day 8, Fall Roundup Bingo: bingo (12 squares for K–2, 16 for 3–5), a read-aloud quiz and Pass the Pencil, with a look back at the month

WHY IT WORKS FOR SUBS
• No tech: nothing needs a computer, projector, speaker or video. Paper, pencils, crayons, hands and voices only
• No music skills: rhythm is taught through speech rhythm, clapping the syllables of words, with the claps printed as dots. The sub never reads music or sings
• No prep: print, put each day in a folder, and go. Each day works in any order for a mixed K–5 class
• Facts are short, sourced and secular, and the facts page answers common questions, so the days work in public schools and for students of every background

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
