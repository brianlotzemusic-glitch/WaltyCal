## 2. Description

```
December Music Bell-Ringers: 20 no-prep daily warm-up pages for grades 3-6. Each page has four short parts that take about 8 minutes: name the notes, write in the counts, match music symbols, and find the missing beats. Snow, mittens, hot cocoa and ice skating keep it winter-themed and non-denominational: no holiday names and no religious symbols, so it fits every school calendar. Answer keys included.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (35 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover (1 page)
• Teacher notes with a 20-day guide (1 page)
• Student reference: treble and bass staff note names, note and rest values, counting (1 page)
• Student reference: a glossary of 21 music symbols (1 page)
• 20 daily warm-up pages, one per day (20 pages)
• Answer keys for all 20 days, two days per page (10 pages)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)

EACH DAILY PAGE
A. Snowy Notes: write the letter name of every note on the treble or bass staff (days 1-12 one clef, days 13-20 both).
B. Mitten Rhythm: write the count under each note and rest, in 4/4, 3/4, 2/4 and 6/8, from quarter notes to eighths and dotted rhythms.
C. Cocoa Symbols: match 5 symbols to their names in a word bank: clefs, rests, sharps and flats, dynamics, fermata, repeat sign, barlines and more.
D. Skate Math: how many beats are missing from the measure?

HOW TO USE
Hand out a page as students arrive or project it as a bell-ringer, warm-up or sub-plan filler. The skills build over the 20 days, and every page is generated so that each measure adds up to its time signature and each key is built from the same data as its page. Great for general music, band, orchestra and choir in December or any time of year.

• Grades 3-6
• Print in black and white; the answer keys use one accent color
• No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
