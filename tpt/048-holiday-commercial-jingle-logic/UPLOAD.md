## 2. Description

```
Commercial music for high school music technology: students act as a jingle writer for an invented product. From one of four fictional client briefs they plan a 30-second ad in bars, choose a tempo, key and chord loop, and build the music in Logic Pro with Drummer, Session Players (if your version has them), Alchemy, Retro Synth, Quick Sampler and library loops. They write and record an 8 to 12 word voice-over, clean it up with EQ, compression and a de-esser, duck the music under it so every word is clear, and export exactly 30 seconds. A classmate then acts as the client and fills in a feedback sheet. This Commercial Jingle project takes 4 class periods and is an original Hudson Beat project; the steps are written for Logic Pro, while the ideas (bars, chord loops, voice-over, ducking) carry over to GarageBand and BandLab.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (ZIP: 28-page PDF = 1 cover + 9 teacher + 17 student + 1 terms page, plus 15-slide intro deck as .pptx and PDF)
• Teacher pages: how to use, project overview, setup, a 4-class pacing guide with the deck slides for each class, two pages of teacher notes (struggles, variations, resources, grading), a Logic quick reference, and a two-page worked example (an invented 30-second ad on paper: a 15-bar plan at 120 BPM with a bar and time table, six layers, a three-line script with its timing and the ducking points) (9 pages)
• Student pages: four invented client briefs with a brief card, a tempo, key and chord-loop page with Roman numerals written out in seven keys, a 30-second plan with a tempo table (bars = BPM ÷ 8), Logic Pro recipes, a layer map, a script page with words, syllables and seconds, voice-over recording and processing, a ducking page, an exact-length bounce page, the 3-page project sheet (overview, goals, vocabulary, requirements, steps, tips, submission checklist), the /25 rubric, a vocabulary page, a client feedback sheet, a feedback planner and a reflection page (17 pages)
• Teacher-presented intro deck, 15 slides with speaker notes on every slide: a think-and-share hook, what the project is, the 30-second plan, the chord loop, a script that fits, ducking, the voice chain, what to turn in and the rubric

THE FOUR CLASS PERIODS (works in 3 or 4 classes)
• Class 1: hook; choose a client brief; pick a tempo, key and loop; plan the 30 seconds in bars
• Class 2: build the music with Drummer, Session Players, Alchemy, Quick Sampler and loops
• Class 3: write and time the script; record and process the voice-over; duck the music; mix
• Class 4: bounce exactly 30 seconds and hand in; client feedback and presentations; reflection

WHAT STUDENTS LEARN
• Planning a short piece to a clock: a bar of 4/4 lasts 240 ÷ BPM seconds, so 30 seconds hold BPM ÷ 8 bars
• Building a chord loop from Roman numerals (capital = major, small = minor) and turning it into a hook
• Writing a script of 8 to 12 words and checking its words, syllables and seconds
• Recording a voice with Channel EQ, Compressor and DeEsser 2, and ducking the music with sidechain or automation
• Taking a client's feedback and explaining their choices in a reflection

GOOD TO KNOW
• There is no audio in this resource, no melody supplied and no student work. Students make every sound and write every tune and word themselves; every product is invented
• The ad season is winter and secular: no carols, holiday songs, real jingles, characters or brands
• Requires Logic Pro on a Mac, headphones and a microphone for the voice-over (the Mac's built-in microphone works). Session Players need a recent Logic Pro; the recipes say what to do without them
• Rubric: 5 categories scored 1-5, /25; the final grade is the average of the student's and the teacher's score
• Grades 9-12; music technology, music production or media music classes
• The deck uses the free Oswald and Source Sans 3 fonts (built into Google Slides); install them for PowerPoint or Keynote, or present the PDF copy, which shows the intended look
• Every student page prints well in black and white

Logic Pro is a product of Apple Inc., named only to describe compatibility. This resource is not affiliated with Apple.

Created with the help of digital and AI tools.
```
