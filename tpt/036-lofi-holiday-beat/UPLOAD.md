## 2. Description

```
Lo-fi beat making for high school music technology: students turn a public-domain carol into a lo-fi hip-hop beat in Logic Pro. They enter the melody and chords from a piano-roll note chart, build a slow, swung drum pattern, and add tape wobble, a Bitcrusher, a low-pass filter and vinyl noise. This Lo-Fi Holiday Beat project takes 4 class periods and is an original Hudson Beat project; the steps are written for Logic Pro, while the ideas (a slow tempo, swing, softened sounds and noise make lo-fi) carry over to GarageBand, Ableton Live and BandLab.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (ZIP: 27-page PDF = 1 cover + 10 teacher + 15 student + 1 terms page, plus 15-slide intro deck as .pptx and PDF)
• Teacher pages: how to use, project overview, setup, a 4-class pacing guide with the deck slides for each class, two pages of teacher notes (struggles, variations, resources, grading), a Logic quick reference, tune sources and the public-domain rule, and a two-page worked example (an invented plan on paper: Good King Wenceslas as an 84-second beat at 80 BPM, with its five lo-fi moves, a 28-bar map, eight layers, chords and three automation lanes) (10 pages)
• Student pages: three piano-roll note charts (Good King Wenceslas, God Rest Ye Merry, Gentlemen and O Tannenbaum in 3/4) with every note listed bar by bar and a blank bass-line roll, four lo-fi drum patterns with grids, two pages of effect cards (swing with a diagram, tape, Bitcrusher, vinyl noise, low-pass filter), a Beat plan, an Arrangement plan with a length helper, the 3-page project sheet (overview, goals, vocabulary, requirements, steps, tips, submission checklist), the /25 rubric, a vocabulary page, a feedback planner and a reflection page (15 pages)
• Teacher-presented intro deck, 15 slides with speaker notes on every slide: a think-and-share hook, what the project is, how to read a piano roll, building the beat, straight against swung, three lo-fi drum patterns, a worked-example map, the four sound moves, what to turn in and the rubric

THE FOUR CLASS PERIODS
• Class 1: hook; choose a carol; fill in the Beat plan; enter the melody and chords
• Class 2: build the beat: drum pattern, swing and a bass line
• Class 3: make it lo-fi with tape, Bitcrusher, a low-pass filter and vinyl noise; plan the form; arrange and mix
• Class 4: bounce and hand in; presentations with feedback questions; reflection

WHAT STUDENTS LEARN
• Entering and editing notes and chords in Logic Pro's Piano Roll from a note chart
• Programming a lo-fi drum pattern and adding swing
• What makes a sound lo-fi: tape wobble, bit crushing, a low-pass filter and vinyl noise, each used small
• Planning a 60 to 90 second arrangement with an intro, beat sections, a break and an outro
• Balancing a mix and explaining their choices in a reflection

GOOD TO KNOW
• There is no audio in this resource and no student work. The three melodies are in the public domain and appear only as note charts, with no lyrics; students make every sound themselves in Logic Pro
• Good King Wenceslas is a winter carol, God Rest Ye Merry, Gentlemen is a Christmas carol and O Tannenbaum is a winter tune. Offer the ones that suit your school
• Requires Logic Pro on a Mac. A MIDI keyboard is not needed; nobody has to read staff notation
• Rubric: 5 categories scored 1-5, /25; the final grade is the average of the student's and the teacher's score
• Grades 9-12; music technology or music production classes
• The deck uses the free Oswald and Source Sans 3 fonts (built into Google Slides); install them for PowerPoint or Keynote, or present the PDF copy, which shows the intended look
• Every student page prints well in black and white

Logic Pro is a product of Apple Inc., named only to describe compatibility. This resource is not affiliated with Apple.

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
