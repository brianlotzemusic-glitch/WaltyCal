Winter Color by Note with three levels in one set, so every student in your class can color the same picture at their own reading level. 9 original, kid-friendly winter and holiday pictures, 27 worksheets and 27 full-color answer keys.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (58 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Teacher notes with a level guide and page list (1 page)

• Level A: Rhythm values (whole, half, quarter, eighth notes and quarter rest), 9 worksheets + 9 answer keys (grades 1–2)
• Level B: Treble clef note names, middle C to A5 with ledger lines, 9 worksheets + 9 answer keys (grades 2–4)
• Level C: Bass clef note names, E2 to middle C with ledger lines, 9 worksheets + 9 answer keys (grades 4–5)

HOW TO USE
Students read the note or rest in each space, find it in the color key, and color the space. In levels B and C every space shows its note on a small staff with a clef, and the key uses letter names. Each worksheet has a matching full-color answer key, and every key is generated from the same data as the worksheet and checked by hand. Great for early finishers, music centers, sub plans and a quick note-reading check in December and January.

• Grades 1–5 (choose the level that fits)
• Print in black and white: every color in the key is also written as a word
• No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters

Designed with the help of digital and AI tools, and checked by hand.
