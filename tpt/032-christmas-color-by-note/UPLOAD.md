## 2. Description

```
Christmas Color by Note with three levels in one set, so every student in your class can color the same picture at their own reading level. 9 original, kid-friendly Christmas pictures, 27 worksheets and 27 full-color answer keys.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (58 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Teacher notes with a level guide and page list (1 page)
• Student reference page: note values and note names on the treble and bass staff (1 page)
• Level A: Rhythm values (whole, half, quarter, eighth notes and quarter rest), 9 worksheets + 9 answer keys (grades 2–3)
• Level B: Treble clef note names, middle C to A5 with ledger lines, 9 worksheets + 9 answer keys (grades 3–5)
• Level C: Bass clef note names, E2 to middle C with ledger lines, 9 worksheets + 9 answer keys (grades 4–6)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)

PICTURES: Christmas tree, Christmas stocking, holly wreath, reindeer, candy canes, jingle bells and holly, candle and holly, Christmas star and holiday hat.

HOW TO USE
Students read the note or rest in each space, find it in the color key, and color the space. In levels B and C every space shows its note on a small staff with a clef, and the key uses letter names. Each worksheet has a matching full-color answer key, and every key is generated from the same data as its worksheet and verified in code. Great for early finishers, music centers, sub plans and December concert weeks.

• Grades 2–6 (choose the level that fits)
• Print in black and white: every color in the key is also written as a word
• No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
