Welcome! This ZIP holds 23 winter and holiday music resources, mostly for elementary music: rhythm and note-name practice, games, color-by-note pages, listening, recorder and drumming play-alongs, and concert-season helpers. Each resource is a ready-to-print PDF in its own numbered folder.

How to use this bundle

  1. Unzip the download. Each resource has its own numbered folder (01 to 23) holding its PDF.
  2. Open the next page: it lists every resource with its grades and page count, so you can pick what fits your classes.
  3. Open a PDF and read its teacher page first. Print the student pages you need; answer keys are inside the PDFs that have them.
  4. Plan your winter weeks: use the concert planner and reflection for concert season, the games and color-by-note pages for Fun Fridays, and the listening unit and holidays-around-the-world pages for a holiday lesson.

What you saved

The 23 resources sold separately add up to $95.25. The bundle price is $62.00: you save $33.25 (34.9%).

Also sold separately

Every resource in this bundle is also sold on its own in the Hudson Beat store on Teachers Pay Teachers, and the files are the same. If you already own some of them, you have them twice: use the folders you need.

Good to know

  • Grade ranges differ by resource: most are for grades 2 to 6, some reach kindergarten or grade 1, and three go beyond elementary: the concert planner (K-8), bucket drumming (3-8) and the concert reflection (4-12, for band, choir and orchestra) (see the table).
  • Holiday resources include Christmas, Hanukkah and winter-general options; use the ones that fit your community.
  • Every page prints well in black and white unless it says otherwise.
