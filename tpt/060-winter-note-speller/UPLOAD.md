## 2. Description

```
Students read a row of notes on the staff, name each note by its letter and write the word the notes spell. Every word uses only the letters A to G, so each note has exactly one answer and the words are real ones such as BAD, CAGE, FADED and DECADE. This set has 64 spell-the-word items in treble clef and bass clef, 12 winter riddles whose answers are spelled in notes, a reference sheet, blank staves for writing your own word, a page for drawing the notes of a given word, and answer keys for every page. Made for general music, band, orchestra and choir in grades 3 to 6.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (20 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover (1 page)
• Teacher notes: levels, a suggested path, ideas for grades 3-4 and grades 5-6 (1 page)
• Note letter reference sheet: lines, spaces, ledger-line notes and the sayings for both clefs (1 page)
• Treble clef spell-the-word worksheets: Level A with notes inside the staff, Level B with ledger-line notes, 8 words per page and 32 words in all (4 pages)
• Bass clef spell-the-word worksheets: Level A and Level B, 8 words per page and 32 words in all (4 pages)
• Winter riddles: 12 riddles in 3 sets, each with a clue, a row of notes and a line for the answer (3 pages)
• Write your own word: 8 blank staves in treble and bass clef (1 page)
• Spell it with notes: 8 given words for students to draw (1 page)
• Answer keys: both clefs, the riddles and the spell-it page (3 pages)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)
Total: 20 pages.

HOW TO USE
Print the pages you need. Students can start with Level A in one clef and move to Level B and the riddles when they are ready. The riddles make a good early-finisher task, and the write-your-own page lets partners trade words.

• Grades 3-6 (grades 3-4 with the reference sheet, grades 5-6 without it)
• Quarter notes on every page, so the pages practice pitch only
• Prints well in black and white; one accent color
• No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
