Teacher Notes

Holiday Music Word Puzzles · 6 word searches and 4 crosswords · grades 3–6

What is included

  • 6 word searches with grids from 12 × 12 to 15 × 15 and 12 to 14 words each. Search 1: across and down. Search 2 adds down-right diagonals, 3 adds up-right diagonals, 4 adds backwards words, and 5 and 6 use all 8 directions.
  • 4 crosswords with numbered across and down clues, each with its letter count.
  • A full answer key for every puzzle: word search solutions are highlighted and crossword solutions are filled in. Page 3 is a how-to page for students.

Ways to use the puzzles

  • Early finishers. Keep a folder of copies at the back of the room. Students take one puzzle, then check it with the key.
  • Centers. Put the puzzles in a center with pencils and highlighters. Partners can work on one puzzle together.
  • Sub plans. Leave a set of puzzles and the answer key pages. No music skills are needed to supervise.

Choosing a puzzle

  • Word Searches 1–2: 1 is across and down, 2 adds down-right diagonals. Grades 3–4.
  • Word Searches 3–4: 3 adds up-right diagonals, 4 adds backwards words. Grades 4–5.
  • Word Searches 5–6: all 8 directions. Grades 5–6.
  • Crosswords 1–4: grades 3–4, 4–5, 3–4 and 5–6.

Grade bands are suggestions. Students who need more support can tick words off the word list as they go.

Skills practiced

Music vocabulary, spelling, careful reading of clues and scanning a grid. The puzzles use instruments of the orchestra and band, music symbols and terms, winter music words, rhythm words and music jobs. No standards codes are listed: match the skills to your own curriculum.

Good to know

The content is winter themed and non-denominational. No songs, lyrics, brands or characters are used.

How to Solve

Read this page first, then pick a puzzle.

Word searches

1

Read the word list. Pick one word and look for its first letter in the grid.

2

Check the letters next to it in every direction the puzzle allows. The word is a straight line.

3

Circle the word and check it off the list. Each word is hidden exactly once.

Example: TEMPO goes across and CLEF goes down.

Harder puzzles use all 8 directions. Words can also run backwards, so look for them from right to left and from bottom to top.

Crosswords

1

Find the number in the grid. Across clues go left to right and down clues go top to bottom.

2

Read the clue and count the letters. The number in brackets tells you how many boxes to fill.

3

Write one letter in each box. Letters in crossing words must match, so use them to check your answers.

Example, solved. Box 1 starts TEMPO across and TIE down.

Across

1 How fast or slow the music goes (5)
3 Italian word that means loud (5)

Down

1 Curved line that joins two notes of the same pitch (3)
2 Italian word that means soft (5)

Tips

  • In a word search, look for rare letters such as X, Y, Z and K first.
  • In a crossword, start with the clues you know and fill in short answers first.
  • When you are stuck, move on. Crossing letters from other answers will help.
