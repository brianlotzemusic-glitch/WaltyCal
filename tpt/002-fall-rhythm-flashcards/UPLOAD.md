## 2. Description

```
Fall rhythm flashcards and tracing worksheets for K–2 music: quarter note, two eighth notes, quarter rest and half note. Every card comes in standard notation and stick notation, with simple leaf, acorn, pumpkin and owl art that prints well in black and white.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (29 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Teacher notes: how to prep and use the cards, game ideas, page guide (1 page)
• Rhythm reference / anchor chart: every symbol with its beats (1 page)
• 8 symbol flashcards: the four symbols in standard and stick notation, 4 per page (2 pages)
• 24 four-beat pattern cards: 12 patterns in 3 sets, standard notation (4/4, one-line rhythm staff) and stick notation, 2 large cards per page (12 pages). Set A: quarter and eighth notes. Set B adds the quarter rest. Set C adds the half note.
• 5 tracing worksheets: one for each symbol (trace big, trace small, then draw your own) plus a 4-beat pattern page (5 pages)
• 3 beat-count worksheets with answer keys: How Many Beats?, Count the Beats, Four Beats or Not? (6 pages)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)

HOW TO USE
Flash a card, count in and clap it. Pat the leaves under each pattern card to feel the steady beat. Lay out a few cards and clap one: which did I clap? Line up symbol cards to compose a 4-beat pattern. Use the tracing pages for centers, early finishers or sub days.

RHYTHM SYLLABLES
Kodály syllables (ta, ti-ti, ta-a) appear only as a small, labelled option on the cards. Many teachers use Takadimi, Gordon, counting or word chants instead, so use whatever your students know. An optional fall word chant is included: leaf, pump-kin, whoo-oo (owl), shh.

• Grades K–2
• Every pattern card is exactly 4 beats; every answer key is generated from the printed patterns and checked by hand
• Prints in black and white
• No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters

Designed with the help of digital and AI tools, and checked by hand.
```
