## 2. Description

```
Trap beat making for high school music technology: students flip a public-domain Tchaikovsky theme into a trap beat in Logic Pro. They enter the melody and chords from a piano-roll note chart, build a half-time drum pattern with hi-hat rolls and an 808 sub bass, and flip the tune by chopping it, jumping an octave or reversing it. This Trap Nutcracker project takes 4 class periods and is an original Hudson Beat project; the steps are written for Logic Pro, while the ideas (a half-time snare, an 808, rolling hats and a flipped melody make trap) carry over to GarageBand, Ableton Live and BandLab.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (ZIP: 27-page PDF = 1 cover + 10 teacher + 15 student + 1 terms page, plus 15-slide intro deck as .pptx and PDF)
• Teacher pages: how to use, project overview, setup, a 4-class pacing guide with the deck slides for each class, two pages of teacher notes (struggles, variations, resources, grading), a Logic quick reference, tune sources and the public-domain rule, and a two-page worked example (an invented plan on paper: the Sugar Plum Fairy as a 64-second beat at 150 BPM, with its five trap moves, a 40-bar map, eight layers, chords and three automation lanes) (10 pages)
• Student pages: two piano-roll note charts (Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairy and Trepak) with every note listed bar by bar and a blank 808 roll, five flips drawn from one bar of Trepak, four half-time drum patterns with grids, two pages of effect cards (hi-hat rolls with a diagram, 808, half-time snare, flipping the melody, riser and drop), a Beat plan, an Arrangement plan with a length helper, the 3-page project sheet (overview, goals, vocabulary, requirements, steps, tips, submission checklist), the /25 rubric, a vocabulary page, a feedback planner and a reflection page (15 pages)
• Teacher-presented intro deck, 15 slides with speaker notes on every slide: a think-and-share hook, what the project is, how to read a piano roll, building the beat, hi-hat rolls, trap drum patterns, a worked-example map, flipping the tune, what to turn in and the rubric

THE FOUR CLASS PERIODS
• Class 1: hook; choose a theme; fill in the Beat plan; enter the melody and chords
• Class 2: build the beat: half-time drums, hi-hat rolls, an 808 and a flipped melody
• Class 3: make it hit with the snare, the 808, a riser and a drop; plan the form; arrange and mix
• Class 4: bounce and hand in; presentations with feedback questions; reflection

WHAT STUDENTS LEARN
• Entering and editing notes and chords in Logic Pro's Piano Roll from a note chart
• Programming a half-time trap drum pattern, with hi-hat rolls and a velocity ramp
• Building an 808 sub bass on the chord roots, with glide and a little distortion
• Flipping a melody (chop, stretch, reverse, octave jump) so it stays recognizable
• Planning a 60 to 90 second arrangement with an intro, beat sections, a break, a drop and an outro, then explaining their choices in a reflection

GOOD TO KNOW
• There is no audio in this resource and no student work. Both themes are in the public domain (Tchaikovsky, 1892) and appear only as note charts, with no lyrics; students make every sound themselves in Logic Pro
• The themes come from the Nutcracker ballet, but neither is a carol and neither has words; offer the ones that suit your school
• Requires Logic Pro on a Mac. A MIDI keyboard is not needed; nobody has to read staff notation
• Rubric: 5 categories scored 1-5, /25; the final grade is the average of the student's and the teacher's score
• Grades 9-12; music technology or music production classes
• The deck uses the free Oswald and Source Sans 3 fonts (built into Google Slides); install them for PowerPoint or Keynote, or present the PDF copy, which shows the intended look
• Every student page prints well in black and white

Logic Pro is a product of Apple Inc., named only to describe compatibility. This resource is not affiliated with Apple.

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
