A FREE color by note sampler: one coloring page at each of three reading levels, with answer keys, so you can try the format with your class before you buy a full set.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (5 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Level A: Rhythm values (whole, half, quarter, eighth notes and quarter rest), sunflower picture (grades 1–2)

• A "See the full set" page with links to the full Color by Note sets, plus terms of use and credits

HOW TO USE
Students read the note or rest in each space, find it in the color key, and color the space. Every color in the key is also written as a word, so the pages print well in black and white. Great for early finishers, music centers, sub plans and a quick note-reading check.

The full sets have 9 pictures at every level (27 worksheets and 27 full-color answer keys each). Follow the Hudson Beat store to hear about new sets.
