Winter Concert PlannerPlanning timeline · 8 weeks to concert day
TIMELINE
Check it off
Write a target date for each stage, then tick each box as you go.
8 weeks out ·Target date
  • Confirm the date, time and room with the office
  • Choose a snow date
  • Choose the music and check you have enough copies
  • Put the concert on the school calendar
6 weeks out ·Target date
  • Send the parent letter (page 12)
  • Start the rehearsal tracker (page 6)
  • Decide on concert attire
  • Ask for volunteers (page 10)
4 weeks out ·Target date
  • Make the seating or riser chart (pages 8–9)
  • Book sound, lights and a piano tuning
  • Draft the program order (page 5)
  • Confirm an accompanist if you need one
2 weeks out ·Target date
  • Send reminder notes home
  • Finish the program and proofread every name
  • Confirm volunteers, custodians and staff helpers
  • Plan the run of show (page 11)
Concert week ·Target date
  • Dress rehearsal in the performance space
  • Print and fold the programs
  • Set up risers, chairs and stands; do a sound check
  • Pack folders, water and a spare-supplies box
Concert day ·Target date
  • Post the run of show backstage
  • Greet students at call time and warm up
  • Welcome families and thank your helpers
  • Enjoy the music!
After the concert ·Target date
  • Send thank-you notes (pages 17–18)
  • Return borrowed equipment
  • Student reflection (page 19)
  • Write notes for next year below

Placement tips ·
  • Tallest singers on the back step, shortest in front.
  • Strong singers in the middle of each section help the others.
  • Leave an end spot free for a student who may need to step out.
