Teacher Notes

Winter Concert Planner + Program Kit · for any K–8 winter concert

What it is

Everything you need to plan and run a winter concert, in one printable file: a planning timeline, repertoire and rehearsal trackers, seating charts, a stage and tech checklist, a run of show, a parent letter, reminder notes, three program layouts, thank-you notes and a student reflection.

How to use it

  1. Print the planner pages (pages 3–11) and keep them in a concert binder or folder.
  2. Start with Concert at a Glance (page 4) and the timeline (page 3). Write a target date next to each stage.
  3. Send the parent letter about six weeks out and the reminder notes a few days before.
  4. Fill in a program, proofread the names, then copy it for families.
  5. After the concert, send thank-you notes and have students complete the reflection.

Filling it in

Every page is designed to be written on by hand. You can also type onto any page with the “add text” tool in most PDF readers before you print.

Printing tips

  • All pages are US Letter and print well in black and white.
  • Program B and the reminder notes are two per page: cut on the dashed line. The thank-you notes are four per page.
  • Programs look great on light blue or gray paper; thank-you notes on card stock.

For every school

The kit is non-denominational: snowflakes, mittens, cocoa, music stands and drums, and nothing tied to a particular celebration. Piece titles are left blank for you, so it works for choir, band, orchestra, general music and whole-school concerts.

Standards

The rehearsal pages and student reflection support refining and presenting work for an audience (National Core Arts Standards, Music, MU:Pr5.1 and MU:Pr6.1).

All layouts, art and wording are original. No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters are used.

Placement tips ·
  • Tallest singers on the back step, shortest in front.
  • Strong singers in the middle of each section help the others.
  • Leave an end spot free for a student who may need to step out.
