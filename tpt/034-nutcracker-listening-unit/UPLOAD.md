## 2. Description

```
A complete Nutcracker listening unit for grades 2-6, built around Tchaikovsky's 1892 ballet. Students read the story in six scenes, then listen to six famous movements with a listening map and response page for each, hunt for instruments, take a 16-point quiz and write their own review. Every answer has a key. No prep beyond choosing a recording: you play the music (no audio is included).

WHAT'S INCLUDED (17 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover and teacher notes with pacing for 6 class periods and tips for choosing a recording (2 pages)
• The Nutcracker story in six scenes, with facts about Tchaikovsky and the ballet (1 page)
• Story check: put the events in order, who is who, draw and tell (1 page)
• Listening toolkit: tempo, dynamics, beat, meter, pitch, timbre, mood and the orchestra families (1 page)
• 6 listening maps with response pages, one per movement (6 pages)
• Instrument gallery with 8 original drawings, and a spotting chart (2 pages)
• 16-point quiz: matching, multiple choice and true/false (1 page)
• Answer key for the story check, spotting chart and quiz (1 page)
• My Nutcracker review page (1 page)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)

THE SIX MOVEMENTS
1. March (guests arrive): loud and soft map
2. Waltz of the Snowflakes: snowflake path, listen for the children's voices
3. Trepak (Russian Dance): speed gauges for a dance that keeps speeding up
4. Dance of the Reed Flutes: short or smooth, count the flutes
5. Waltz of the Flowers: ONE-two-three groups, listen for the harp
6. Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairy: draw the tune and find the celesta

WHY TEACHERS LIKE IT
• One routine for every movement: just listen, listen with the map, then circle and describe
• Grades 2-3 finish a sentence; grades 4-6 write three sentences with the toolkit words
• Works for a December music class, a winter program, a sub day or a ballet field trip
• Prints well in black and white
• All pages, maps and instrument art are original. The music is public domain; no recordings are included, so you choose one you can play in class

Created with the help of digital and AI tools. Notation and answer keys are verified before release.
```
