Billboard Top 10Project sheet · requirements and steps
STUDENT
2 of 3
NameDate

Requirements ·

Choose one song from the current Billboard Top 10
Match the tempo of your chosen song exactly
Recreate the exact song form (intro, verse, chorus, bridge, etc.)
Drum patterns must be self-created — percussion loops are okay as accents
Use appropriate instruments and layers for the genre
Apply EQ and plug-ins to shape your sounds authentically

Steps / Process ·

  1. 1
    Explore the Billboard Top 10 — listen to all 10 songs and complete the Top 10 analysis (page 11)
  2. 2
    Study song form — map out the exact structure of your chosen song using the Song Form map (page 12)
  3. 3
    Analyze and program drum patterns — break down the kick, snare, and hi-hat and build them in Logic (page 13). Set up Arrangement Markers.
  4. 4
    Build your foundation — lay down bass, chords, and core instrumentation to match the original’s energy
  5. 5
    Add layers — fill in melodies, pads, FX, and transitions to match the fullness of the original
  6. 6
    Mix and refine — EQ every track, pan for width, adjust levels, and add plug-ins for authenticity
  7. 7
    Present & Get Feedback — Create your own Padlet post with 3 questions you want classmates to answer about your project. Present to the class. During classmates’ presentations, answer at least 2 of their 3 questions on their Padlet post.
