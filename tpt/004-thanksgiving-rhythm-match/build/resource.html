Teacher Notes

Thanksgiving Rhythm Match + Echo Cards · grades 1–3 · ta, ti-ti, rest, ta-a

What it is

Students say short Thanksgiving word phrases (“Pump-kin pie, pump-kin pie”), clap them, and match each one to its rhythm in standard notation. Echo cards build the ear: you perform, the class echoes.

How the words work

  • Every phrase is exactly 4 beats, one box per beat.
  • One sound in a beat = ta (quarter note). Two sounds = ti-ti (two eighth notes).
  • “shh” = a silent beat (quarter rest).
  • An arrow = hold the word for two beats (half note, ta-a).
  • Stressed syllables fall on the beat, so the words sound natural when spoken.

Two levels

  • Set 1 (cards 1–12, A–L): ta and ti-ti only. Good for grade 1 and early grade 2.
  • Set 2 (cards 13–24, M–X): adds the quarter rest and half note. Grades 2–3.
  • Echo cards: Level 1 ta/ti-ti (#1–8), Level 2 adds the rest (#9–16), Level 3 adds the half note (#17–24).

Getting ready

  • Print the card pages on card stock. Print each set’s word and rhythm pages on different colored paper if you like, so the two piles stay apart.
  • Cut on the dashed lines. Laminate for years of use.

Syllables

Cards use Kodály-style syllables (ta, ti-ti, ta-a) and “shh” for the rest. If your class uses another system (Takadimi, Gordon, counting 1 & 2 &), say that instead; the notation is the same.

Music notes

Every rhythm is one bar of 4/4 on a one-line rhythm staff, stems up, eighth notes beamed in pairs, spaced one beat per slot. Half notes start on beat 1 or 3. Every match pattern is different, so each word card has exactly one rhythm card.

Standards

Supports reading and performing rhythm patterns in iconic and standard notation (National Core Arts Standards, Music, MU:Pr4.2, grades 1–3) and echoing rhythm patterns by ear.

Harvest theme: turkey, pies, corn, apples, leaves, acorns and pumpkins. All words, rhythms and art are original. No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters are used.

How to Use

Say it · clap it · read it · echo it

Rhythm match games

  • Partner match: lay out one set face up. Partners say and clap a word card, then find its rhythm card. Check with the key (page 17).
  • Memory: one set face down in a grid (24 cards). Turn over two; keep them if the words match the rhythm. Students must clap the pair to keep it.
  • Find your partner: give half the class word cards and half rhythm cards. Students walk, clap and say, and pair up. Each pair performs for the class.
  • Pocket chart: post the rhythm cards; draw a word card and the class decides together where it goes.
  • Around the room: tape the rhythm cards to the walls; students carry a word card and hunt for its rhythm.

Worksheets

  • Match It! 1 and 2 (pages 9–10): draw lines from words to rhythms.
  • Write the Rhythm (page 11): six new phrases; students write the notes or stick notation.
  • Compose (page 12): open-ended; students build and perform their own 4-beat chant.

Echo cards

  1. Keep a steady beat (pat, tap or a drum). Count in: “1, 2, ready, echo.”
  2. Perform the card. The class echoes it right away, keeping the beat going.
  3. Show the card and say the syllables together.

Echo ideas

  • Echo or not? Perform the card correctly or with one beat changed; students thumbs-up or thumbs-down.
  • Which card? Show three cards, perform one; students point to it.
  • Student leader: a student draws a card and leads the echo.
  • Word swap: put Thanksgiving words to the echo rhythm (ti-ti = tur-key, ta = pie).
  • Instruments: echo on drums, rhythm sticks or one pitch on a xylophone.

Tips

  • Speak the words in rhythm with the beat before clapping.
  • In Set 2, show the rest as a quiet gesture (open hands) so students feel the silent beat.
  • Collect cards by set so the class sets stay together.

Words into rhythm · one box is one beat

“Pump-kin” has two sounds in one beat (ti-ti). “Pie” has one sound (ta). ti-ti ta ti-ti ta = 4 beats.

“Gob!” is held for two beats, so it is a half note (ta-a). ti-ti ti-ti ta-a = 4 beats.

  • Count the sounds in each beat box: 1 sound = ta, 2 sounds = ti-ti, no sound = rest.
  • Every card and echo pattern is 4 beats, one bar of 4/4 time.
