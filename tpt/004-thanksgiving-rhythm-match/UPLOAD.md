## 2. Description

```
Thanksgiving rhythm practice for grades 1–3 music: students say short harvest phrases like "Pump-kin pie, pump-kin pie", clap them, and match each one to its rhythm in standard notation. Then build their ears with 24 clap-back echo cards. Turkey, pie, corn, apple, leaf, acorn and pumpkin art; no history scenes or religious images.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (21 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Teacher notes and How to Use: games, echo ideas, tips (2 pages)
• Rhythm reference / anchor chart: ta, ti-ti, quarter rest and ta-a, each with a Thanksgiving word example (1 page)
• 24 word cards + 24 rhythm cards for matching, in two levels, 12 per page (4 pages)
• 4 worksheets: Match It! 1 and 2 (draw a line), Write the Rhythm (six new phrases), Compose Your Own with a word bank (4 pages)
• 24 echo cards in three levels, 6 per page, with syllables under every beat (4 pages)
• Answer keys: the match game and all three closed worksheets (4 pages)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)

TWO MATCH LEVELS
• Set 1 (cards 1–12, A–L): ta and ti-ti only
• Set 2 (cards 13–24, M–X): adds the quarter rest and the half note
Every phrase is exactly 4 beats, written one box per beat, so students count the sounds in each beat: 1 sound = ta, 2 sounds = ti-ti, "shh" = a silent beat, an arrow = hold for two beats. Every rhythm card is different, so each word card has exactly one match. Rhythm cards are lettered in mixed-up order, so the letter never gives the answer away.

ECHO CARDS
• Level 1 (#1–8): ta and ti-ti
• Level 2 (#9–16): adds the rest
• Level 3 (#17–24): adds the half note
Perform a card, and the class echoes it right back. Ideas included: Echo or Not?, Which Card?, student leader, word swap and instruments.

GAME IDEAS
Partner match, memory, find your partner, pocket chart and around-the-room hunts.

• Grades 1–3
• Every rhythm is one bar of 4/4 on a one-line rhythm staff
• Kodály-style syllables (ta, ti-ti, ta-a); use any counting system your students know
• Prints well in black and white
• All phrases, rhythms and art are original. No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters

Designed with the help of digital and AI tools, and checked by hand.
```
