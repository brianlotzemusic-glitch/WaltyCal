# Visual audit of all live products, 15 Nov 2026

Six independent Sonnet inspectors looked at cover.png and preview-1..3.png of the 42 live items at full size (the new "Visual inspector
brief" in TPT-FACTORY.md). These are their RAW findings: **unverified**. The fix agent confirms each one by looking at the image before
changing anything, lists any false alarm in its reply, and leaves false alarms alone.

Tiers. **P1** = wrong content (a teacher could be misled): fix first. **P2** = visible flaw or artefact in the product or the buyer
images. **P3** = layout polish: fix only when the item is being rebuilt anyway; never rebuild a product for P3 alone.
Where a defect is on a PDF page, the PDF is replaced too. Where it is only in cover/preview images, only those are rebuilt.
