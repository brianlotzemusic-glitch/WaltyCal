## 2. Description

```
Winter Concert Reflection and Self-Assessment: 14 printable pages for grades 4-12 that help band, choir and orchestra students look back on a concert, score their own work with a 4-level rubric and set a goal for next time. The wording is non-denominational, so it fits any school's winter or December concert.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (14 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover (1 page)
• Teacher Guide (2 pages)
• My Concert (1 page)
• Self-Assessment Checklists: Band, Choir, Orchestra (3 pages)
• 4-Level Rubric (1 page)
• Concert Etiquette Reflection (1 page)
• Looking Back: grades 4-6 and grades 7-12 (2 pages)
• Goal-Setting Sheet (1 page)
• Class Summary and Tally Sheet (1 page)
• Terms of Use and Credits (1 page)

HOW IT WORKS
Students fill in a My Concert page, tick statements on a checklist for their ensemble, and turn the ticks in each of six skill areas (tone, rhythm, pitch and intonation, dynamics, teamwork, concert etiquette) into a level from 1 to 4. The six levels add up to a total from 6 to 24, with four score bands. Each ensemble checklist has 18 tick statements (54 across the three versions). The etiquette page has 10 statements. Students then finish a Looking Back page, a goal sheet with a SMART goal, next steps and a practice plan.

FOR TEACHERS
The teacher guide explains scoring with a worked example, differentiation for grades 4-6 and grades 7-12, and conferencing tips. Use the rubric to score the same six areas yourself and compare. The class summary sheet has rows for 20 students, class averages and band counts. About 45 minutes of student time in all.

• Grades 4-12
• Print in black and white
• No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters
• Answer Key: Not Included (the rubric and worked example cover scoring)

Created with the help of digital and AI tools.
```
