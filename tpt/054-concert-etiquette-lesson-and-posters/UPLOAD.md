## 2. Description

```
Get your students ready to be a great audience, and a great ensemble, with a 23-minute concert etiquette mini-lesson. This printable pack has a teacher script, 5 discussion questions, a think-pair-share, a "show me" role-play with no instruments, 6 classroom posters, an audience checklist, a performer checklist and a 3-question exit ticket with an answer key. It works for band, orchestra, choir and general music in grades 3 to 8, and nothing in it is tied to a season or a holiday.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (17 pages, PDF, US Letter)
• Cover (1 page)
• Teacher notes: how to use the pack before a concert, differentiation for grades 3-5 and grades 6-8, and extension ideas (1 page)
• Lesson at a glance: 6 steps that add up to 23 minutes (1 page)
• Teacher script: what to say and do for each step, 5 discussion questions and 4 role-play scenarios (2 pages)
• Think-pair-share sheet (1 page)
• 6 printable posters, one per page: "Arrive early and quietly", "Silence phones", "Clap at the right time", "Stay seated until the music ends", "Be a respectful audience" and "Performers: ready, focused, kind" (6 pages)
• Audience checklist with 8 audience items and tick boxes (1 page)
• Performer checklist with 8 performer items and tick boxes (1 page)
• Exit ticket with 3 exit ticket questions, 2 tickets to a page (1 page)
• Exit ticket answer key (1 page)
• Terms of use and credits (1 page)
Total: 17 pages.

HOW TO USE
Teach the mini-lesson a week or so before a concert, post the 6 posters, and hand out the checklists on the day. After the concert, students tick the boxes and write one thing that went well. The pack states plainly that concert customs vary by venue, style and culture, and it invites students to watch and listen for cues. It also includes ideas for including every student.

• No songs, lyrics or copyrighted characters
```
