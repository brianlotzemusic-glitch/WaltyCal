**Description**
Cosy cottagecore Christmas: six original spotted toadstool ornament cut files for the tree, gift tags and the window.

Each ornament stars a classic red-and-white style fly agaric toadstool: three toadstools on a snowy hill under hanging stars, a toadstool cottage with round windows, an arched door and a lantern, a classic bauble with toadstools cut out of it among snowflakes, a woodland gnome in a floppy hat beside a big toadstool, toadstools growing on a crescent moon, and a snow globe with toadstools and a pine. Big spotted caps keep every design easy to read, even small on the tree.

Each design is a single-layer, single-path cut file with a hanging loop built in, so no slicing, welding or cleanup is needed. Stars, trees and toadstools are attached, so nothing falls out.

SIZING TIP
Default size is about 6 in on the longest side; resize freely with the proportions locked. The star threads and the fine cut line around the cottage door are the thinnest parts (about 0.08 in at 6 in), so we recommend 4 in or larger for cardstock, felt and vinyl and 4.5 in or larger for laser-cut wood or acrylic. Do a test cut on a new material first.
