BOLD & EASY
  Thick outlines (about 2 mm when printed) and big, closed spaces, so
  the pages work with crayons, colored pencils, markers and paint, for
  children and for grown-ups who want a relaxing, low-detail page.
  There is no text on the pages and each design has its own page.

PRINTING TIPS
  Print at 100% / "actual size", not "fit to page" (fit to page also
  works, the art just gets a little smaller).
  Each design sits inside a rounded frame with a white margin of at
  least 14 mm, so home printers will not cut it off.
  Plain printer paper is fine for crayons and pencils. For markers or
  paint, use thicker paper or card stock (about 65-110 lb / 176-300 gsm)
  so the color does not bleed through.
  You can print a single page (choose the page number in your printer
  dialog) or all 20.
