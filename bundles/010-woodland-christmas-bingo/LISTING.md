**Description**
A cozy woodland twist on Christmas bingo: foxes, owls, robins, hedgehogs, snowy cabins, mittens and mugs of cocoa, all drawn in a warm dusk palette of plum, pine, berry and gold. Print it tonight and play at your family Christmas party, a classroom holiday party, a church or club get-together, or a quiet night in.

Every one of the 30 cards is different. Each card has 24 of the 30 woodland pictures and a FREE space, no two cards have the same set of pictures, and no two cards share a winning row, column or diagonal. Every picture is on 24 of the 30 cards, so every player has the same chance. The pictures are big and clear, so children who can't read yet can play (and even be the caller).

WHAT'S INCLUDED
• 30 unique 5×5 bingo cards, 2 per page (cut each page in half)
• 30 calling cards to cut apart and draw from a bowl or stocking
• Caller's checklist to tick off each picture as it's called
• 72 solid gold bingo markers to cut out: enough for 3 players at blackout, so print the page once for every 3 players (or use candy, buttons or mini marshmallows)
• How to play page with 5 ways to win (any line, four corners, snowy X, picture frame and blackout) and a tie rule
• 2 pdf files: US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) and A4, 20 pages each

THE 30 PICTURES
Fox, owl, stag, robin, pine tree, mushroom, lantern, mitten, star, sled, cabin, snowflake, acorn, holly, pinecone, hedgehog, rabbit, wreath, candle, cocoa, scarf, gift, moon, bird house, squirrel, bear, stocking, bell, snowman and ice skate.

GOOD TO KNOW
• For 2 to 30 players, ages 3 and up
• Ties can happen, especially in blackout games. The instructions include a simple rule: if two players call BINGO on the same picture, both win, or play one quick "any line" round
• White backgrounds and light colours, so it's easy on your ink
• Print at 100% (actual size) on plain paper or card stock; slip cards into sheet protectors and use dry-erase markers to reuse them every year
