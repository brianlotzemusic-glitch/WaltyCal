**Description**
Set a cozy Thanksgiving table without the shopping trip. These printable place cards fold into little tents and feature a fox, hedgehog, squirrel, owl, wild turkey, deer, acorns, an oak leaf, wheat, mushrooms, a maple leaf and an apple, each with a tartan, gingham, stripe or polka dot band in warm autumn colors. Print at home, cut, fold, write a name and stand it up.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
• 12 folded place cards in 12 different designs, 4 per page on 3 pages (3.5 x 2 in when folded)
• A name line on both sides, so guests across the table can read it too
• 4 menu tent cards for the buffet: Mains, Sides, Desserts and Drinks, each with write-in lines
• Grey cut lines and a dashed fold line on every card
• A print, cut and fold guide page
• Each PDF in US Letter (8.5 x 11 in) and A4, 5 pages each

HOW TO USE
• Print at 100% (actual size) on white or cream card stock, about 160 to 250 gsm (65 to 110 lb)
• Cut along the grey line with scissors, or a paper trimmer for straight edges
• Score the dashed line with a ruler and an empty pen, then fold in half
• Write the guest's name on the line with a pen, gel pen or paint pen, and stand it up
• Fonts are embedded, so the pages print the same everywhere

GOOD TO KNOW
• The cards use full color, so expect normal ink use for a colored page
• Colors can look slightly different on screen and on paper
• The names are written by hand, so nothing needs editing on a screen
• Printing and card stock are not included
