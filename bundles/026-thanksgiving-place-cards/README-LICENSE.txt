HOW TO PRINT AND MAKE
1. Print at 100% / "actual size" (not "fit to page") on white or cream card stock, about 160-250 gsm (65-110 lb).
2. Cut along the grey line with scissors, or a paper trimmer / craft knife and ruler for straight edges.
3. Score the dashed fold line with a ruler and an empty pen, then fold the card in half with the pattern band on the fold.
4. Write the guest's name on the line with a pen, gel pen or paint pen. Both sides are the same, so write on both. Stand it up.
