**Description**
Wrap your presents in something with a little woodland magic. These printable Christmas gift tags have a fox, owl, stag, robin, hedgehog, rabbit, toadstools, a pine branch, a luna moth, holly, mistletoe and a crescent moon, each framed in cozy tartan, gingham, stripes, dots or kraft. Print at home, cut, punch a hole and tie on with twine.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
• 12 large tags (2.5 x 4 in) with a greeting plus To and From lines, 6 per page on 2 pages
• 12 small tags (1.75 x 2.75 in) with To and From lines, all on 1 page
• Five tag shapes (luggage, arch, rounded, scalloped, bevelled) in 12 different looks
• Hole-punch ring marks and grey cut lines on every tag
• A print and make guide page
• Each PDF in US Letter (8.5 x 11 in) and A4, 4 pages each

HOW TO USE
• Print at 100% (actual size) on white or cream card stock, about 160 to 250 gsm (65 to 110 lb)
• Cut along the grey line with scissors, or a paper trimmer for straight edges
• Punch the hole at the ring mark (a 1/4 in / 6 mm punch is ideal)
• Write To and From, thread twine or ribbon through the hole and tie it on
• Fonts are embedded, so the pages print the same everywhere

GOOD TO KNOW
• The tags use full color, so expect normal ink use for a colored page
• Colors can look slightly different on screen and on paper
• Printing, card stock and string are not included
