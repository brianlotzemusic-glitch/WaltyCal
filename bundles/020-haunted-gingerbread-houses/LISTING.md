**Description**
Sweet meets spooky: six original haunted gingerbread house cut files for a Gothmas mantel, a Halloween shelf or a creepy-cute Christmas window.

Every house is a proper gingerbread house, with thick iced roofs, dripping or scalloped piped icing, gumdrops and candy canes, gone a little haunted: crooked walls, a witch-hat tower, a skull on the roof, a coffin door, a spider web window, a jack-o'-lantern, bats, a crescent moon, dead trees, tombstones and an iron fence.

Four designs are standing houses, each on a flat base strip, so they stand up on their own when cut from 3 mm wood or acrylic or from heavy cardstock. One is a wide haunted village panel for window decals, light-box fronts or mantel silhouettes, and one is a round hanging ornament with a loop. Windows, doors and icing lines are cut-outs that glow when a tea light is behind them.

Each design is a single-layer, single-path cut file, so no slicing, welding or cleanup is needed. The bats, moon, chimney smoke and dangling spider are attached, so nothing falls out.

SIZING TIP
Default size is about 6 in on the longest side; resize freely with the proportions locked. For the standing houses and the ornament we recommend 4 in or larger in cardstock and 5 in or larger in wood or acrylic. The village panel has small windows, so use it at 10 in wide or larger for vinyl and cardstock and 12 in or larger for laser-cut wood. Do a test cut on a new material first.
