**Description**
A snowy little village for your window, mantel or shelf: six original Christmas village silhouettes with cosy cottages, a starlit chapel, a gingerbread house, a log cabin and snowy pines.

Two designs are wide panels: a village street skyline with a chapel, cottages and pines, and a framed light-box front with cottages under a crescent moon and falling snow. Use them as window decals, light-box or shadow-box fronts, or mantel and shelf silhouettes. The other four are single buildings, each on a flat base strip, so they stand up on their own when cut from 3 mm wood or acrylic or from heavy cardstock. Windows, doors, snowflakes and icing details are cut-outs that glow when a light is behind them.

Each design is a single-layer, single-path cut file, so no slicing, welding or cleanup is needed. The chimney smoke, the chapel star and the bell are attached, so nothing falls out.
