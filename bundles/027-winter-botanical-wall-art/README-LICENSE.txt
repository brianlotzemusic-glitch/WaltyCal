QUALITY NOTE
The artwork is about 3200 x 4100 px at its core. JPGs are 300 DPI up to roughly 11 x 14 in. Larger sizes are high-resolution enlargements: fine from normal viewing distance, softer up close. The PDFs are lighter (about 80 to 130 DPI at their largest size, more at smaller sizes) and are best for print shops that scale to fit or for smaller sizes; use the JPGs when you want the best quality.

PRINTING TIPS
- Print at 100% (actual size), not "fit to page", when the paper matches the file size. For Letter or A4 paper use the 4:5 or ISO files and "fit to page".
- Matte or satin photo paper (about 200 gsm) shows the deep blue best. A print shop or online photo lab can print any of the sizes above.
- Colors can look different on screen and on paper, and between printers.
- Frames, mats and printing are not included.
