**Description**
Twenty cozy mushroom and woodland coloring pages with thick, bold outlines and big, easy spaces to fill. Print them tonight and color with your kids, your class, or on your own with a cup of tea: a happy frog on a spotted toadstool, a fox asleep in the ferns, a snail beside tall mushrooms, a little mushroom cottage, a mushroom teapot and fifteen more.

Bold and easy means thick outlines (about 2 mm when printed), every space closed and big enough for crayons, colored pencils and markers, and no tiny fiddly details. Great for little hands, and relaxing for grown-ups who want a calm, low-detail page. There is no text on the pages and each design has its own page.
