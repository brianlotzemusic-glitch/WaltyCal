HOW TO USE
• Print at 100% (actual size) on white or cream card stock, about 160 to 250 gsm (65 to 110 lb)
• Cut along the grey line with scissors, or a paper trimmer for straight edges
• Punch the circle on each tag and thread on twine or ribbon
• Write your note on the To and From lines with a pen, gel pen or paint pen
• Fonts are embedded, so the pages print the same everywhere
