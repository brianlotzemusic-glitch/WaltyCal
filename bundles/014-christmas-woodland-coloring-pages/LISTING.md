**Description**
Twenty cozy Christmas and winter woodland coloring pages with thick, bold outlines and big, easy spaces to fill. Print them tonight and color with your kids, your class, or on your own with a mug of cocoa: a fox in a striped scarf, an owl under a crescent moon, a hedgehog with hot chocolate, a lantern-lit cabin in the snow and sixteen more.

Bold and easy means thick outlines (about 2 mm when printed), every space closed and big enough for crayons, colored pencils and markers, and no tiny fiddly details. Great for little hands, and relaxing for grown-ups who want a calm, low-detail page. There is no text on the pages and each design has its own page.
