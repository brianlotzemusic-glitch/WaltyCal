EACH CHART PAGE HAS
  - a large grid with a symbol in every stitched square (plus the colour
    fill on the colour charts), bold lines every 10 stitches, row and
    column numbers, and red arrows marking the centre
  - the stitch count and finished size on 14-count and 18-count
  - the floss key: symbol, DMC number, colour name, stitch count and a
    skein estimate

STITCHING
  2 strands of floss on 14-count Aida, full cross stitches only. No
  backstitch and no fractional stitches. Start at the centre arrows.
  White, antique white or cream Aida all work; about 6 x 6 in of fabric
  per ornament (a fat quarter, about 18 x 21 in, cuts 9 pieces). Finishing steps (flat felt-backed ornament or 3 in mini
  hoop) are on page 3.

PRINTING TIPS
  Print at 100% / "actual size". Black-and-white printing works too:
  every colour has its own symbol.
