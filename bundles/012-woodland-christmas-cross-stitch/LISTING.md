**Description**
Twelve little woodland ornaments to stitch for the tree, gift tags or a cozy winter hoop: a fox, a snowy owl, a stag, a robin on holly, a toadstool, a little pine, a sleepy moon with stars, a holly sprig, a snowflake, a glowing lantern, a snowy cottage and an acorn with oak leaves. Each one is small and quick, about 2 to 2.5 in on 14-count Aida, so you can finish one in an evening.

Every ornament comes as two charts: a colour version using 3 to 6 DMC colours from one shared palette of 10 (plum, pine green, berry red, moon gold, cream and warm browns), and a one-colour version you can stitch in DMC 3371 or any single shade you love, for a classic sampler look.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
• 28-page pdf pattern in US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) and A4
• Cover and contents page showing all 12 ornaments in both versions
• 12 colour charts and 12 one-colour charts, one ornament per page
• How to stitch and finish as an ornament (felt-backed flat ornament or 3 in mini hoop)
• Floss shopping list with DMC numbers, names and skein totals

EACH CHART PAGE
• Large, easy-to-read grid with a symbol in every square (plus colour fill on the colour charts)
• Bold lines every 10 stitches, row and column numbers, centre arrows
• Stitch count and finished size on 14-count and 18-count
• Floss key: symbol, DMC number, colour name, stitches and skein estimate

STITCHING
• 2 strands on 14-count Aida, full cross stitches only: no backstitch, no fractional stitches
• Stitch counts from 25 × 25 to 32 × 34; about 1.4 to 1.9 in on 18-count
• Works on white, antique white or cream Aida; about 6 × 6 in of fabric per ornament
• Beginner friendly: every chart prints clearly in black and white too
