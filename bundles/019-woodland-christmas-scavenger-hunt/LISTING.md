**Description**
A cozy woodland Christmas scavenger hunt for kids: 15 rhyming clue cards that send little hunters racing from the Christmas tree to the couch, the boots by the door and the bookshelf, all the way to a hidden treasure. Every card has a big woodland picture (fox, owl, robin, squirrel, snowman and more) in a warm dusk palette of plum, pine, berry and gold. Print it tonight and play on Christmas Eve, at a holiday party, in the classroom, or to hand over one special gift.

HOW IT WORKS
Each clue is a short rhyme that describes a spot in your home. You hide each clue at the spot the clue before it describes, hand the kids clue No. 1, and they solve one rhyme after another until they find the treasure and the "Hooray!" card. Put the clues in any order, skip any spot you don't have, or write your own on the blank cards. The answers are only on the answer key page; each card just has a small letter, so nothing gets spoiled.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
• 15 rhyming clue cards, 4 per page, with a "No." circle to number your order
• 1 "Hooray! You found the treasure" card for the prize
• 4 blank clue cards to write your own
• Answer key and hunt planner: the answer, a hiding tip and a tick box for every card
• Hunt tracker: kids colour a stepping stone on a woodland path for every clue they find
• How to play page with set-up steps, tips and party ideas
• 2 pdf files: US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) and A4, 8 pages each

THE 15 HIDING SPOTS
Christmas tree, oven (on the handle, never inside), fridge, mitten and hat drawer, bed, bathtub (empty and dry), boots by the door, couch, bookshelf, washing machine, window sill, doormat, pantry, toy box and Christmas stockings.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Ages 3 to 10: read the rhymes aloud for little ones, or let older kids race in teams
• All 15 clues take about 20 to 30 minutes; use 5 or 6 for toddlers
• White backgrounds, so it's easy on your ink
• Print at 100% (actual size) on plain paper or card stock and keep the cards for next year
