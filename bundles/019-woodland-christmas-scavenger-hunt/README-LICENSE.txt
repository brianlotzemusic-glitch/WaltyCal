THE 15 HIDING SPOTS (letters match the small letter on each card)
  A Christmas tree    B Oven (on the handle, never inside)   C Fridge
  D Mitten and hat drawer   E Bed   F Bathtub (empty and dry)
  G Boots by the door   H Couch   I Bookshelf   J Washing machine (on top)
  K Window sill   L Doormat   M Pantry or snack cupboard   N Toy box
  O Christmas stockings
  Blank cards are lettered P, Q, R and S.

HOW TO SET UP
  1. Print pages 3-7 and cut along the dashed lines.
  2. Choose the clues that fit your home; skip any spot you don't have,
     or write your own clue on a blank card.
  3. Put the clues in any order. Write 1, 2, 3... in the "No." circle on
     each card and in the Order column of the answer key.
  4. Keep clue No. 1 to hand out. Hide every other clue at the spot that
     the clue BEFORE it describes. (If No. 1 is the couch rhyme, hide No. 2
     under a couch cushion.)
  5. Hide the treasure with the "Hooray!" card at the spot your last clue
     describes. Then hand the hunters clue No. 1!

  All 15 clues take about 20-30 minutes. For ages 3-5 use 5 or 6 clues and
  read them aloud. For older kids, add a timer or print two sets and race
  in teams.

PRINTING TIPS
  Print at 100% / "actual size", not "fit to page".
  Card stock (65-110 lb / 176-300 gsm) makes sturdy cards to reuse each year.
  White backgrounds keep ink use low.
