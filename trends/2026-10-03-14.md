## Judge's verdicts (3 Oct 2026)

Searches/fetches used: 11. Etsy itself could not be searched directly, so competition figures below are from web results and RankHero, not live Etsy counts.

### 1. "A little bit obsessed" hobby set: REJECTED
- **IP (fails):** this is not an original sound. "I think I'm a little bit obsessed" is a repeated lyric in Malcolm Todd's song "Obsessica" (2026, album *Do That Again*; songfacts.com lyrics page, checked 3 Oct). HeyOrca (2 Oct) labels the clip "original sound", but the words are the song's hook, so the trend product would be selling a lyric. Using the shortened "a little bit obsessed" would still be the lyric the buyer recognises.
- **Trademark search:** "a little bit obsessed" trademark returned no registration or pending filing for the phrase (only unrelated OBSESSION / BE OBSESSED WITH SUCCESS marks). That doesn't help, because the lyric rule fails on its own.
- **Demand:** no Etsy listings or buyer requests turned up for the phrase. The evidence is one roundup mention.
- Note: generic hobby mugs ("mushroom obsessed" and so on) without the lyric are an evergreen idea, not a trend. They could go to the normal queue some other time.

### 2. Pumpkin Head dance SVG bundle + "Pumpkin head season" tee: REJECTED
- **IP (caution):** the trend's sound is the Disney *Hocus Pocus* line (NewEngen, 30 Sep), so the trend is tied to it. "Pumpkin head season" text is also risky. A search turned up a PUMPKINHEAD mark owned by Bernard T. Wisner covering t-shirts, sweatshirts and caps (Justia; its live/dead status couldn't be checked because the page returned 403), and *Pumpkinhead* is a horror-film franchise. A registered or pending mark for apparel would be enough to reject on its own, and this one is unverified.
- **Demand (fails):** this is a photo-prop trend. People buy a real pumpkin, not a tee or a cut file. No buyer intent showed up for dancing pumpkin-head SVGs. The category is crowded: "pumpkin svg" has about 57k Etsy listings against about 2.4k searches a month (RankHero, Jul 2026), far over the 5k limit, and the "dancing figure" angle isn't clear enough to stand out. Dancing pumpkin-skeleton tees are already common on Walmart and eBay.
- **Lifespan / makeable:** the earliest listing slot is 5 Oct or later (4 Oct is full with POD). Crafters also need time to cut and press before 31 Oct, so it would sell for about 2-3 weeks. That just passes, but the demand and IP failures decide it.

### 3. "Be OK" office chaos mug/tee ("Please see attached (nothing is attached)"): REJECTED
- **IP:** the phrase is generic and not from the song (the sound is Ingrid Michaelson's "Be OK"; NewEngen, 30 Sep). Trademark search for "please see attached": no registration or pending filing found for mugs or apparel. It passes IP as long as we never use the song.
- **Demand (fails):** the trend is a caption format, and each creator writes their own disaster. NewEngen's example is just one caption, not a phrase that buyers are asking for. No Etsy listings or "where can I buy" requests turned up for the phrase. A happy dance format doesn't create buyer intent for one office joke.
- **Lifespan (weak):** the format lasts 2-4 weeks from late September. After the first free slot (5 Oct+) and 2-5 days of POD production, the trend tie-in will be mostly gone. Without the trend, it's an ordinary office-humour mug, which is off the Duskwood look and doesn't need a [trend] slot.

**Result: 0 approved.** Nothing was added to queue.md. All three picks rest on one marketing roundup each, with no measured Etsy demand. Seasonal ideas already in the queue (Gothmas, nutcracker, celestial Christmas) cover October demand better.
