## Candidates

### 1. "Kinda chic to..." autumn homebody variant (TikTok + Instagram)
- **What:** Caption format "Kinda chic to [unglamorous thing]". It started as body-positivity/wellbeing (Apr-Aug 2026) and is now being reused for autumn: Pepper's example is "Kinda chic to cancel plans because it's raining".
- **Evidence:** Pepper, 1 Oct 2026 (listed as a current October format). Older context: KYM 14 Aug, Hola 18 Aug (searches "up 5,000%", celebrity posts). So it is a months-old format with a fresh seasonal turn, not a brand-new one. Moderate.
- **Buyers:** homebodies, introverts, readers, cozy-season fans buying for themselves or as a "this is so you" gift.
- **Product:** tee/mug/sticker in our own wording: "Kinda chic to stay in when it rains" (candle, teapot, sleeping fox), "Kinda chic to be a night owl" (owl on a crescent moon), "Kinda chic to forage instead of socialise" (mushrooms). One SVG set of 6 phrase+motif designs.
- **Etsy competition (estimate):** low hundreds for "kinda chic"; no listings surfaced in web search.
- **Lifespan:** format 4-8 more weeks; the homebody phrases sell through winter.
- **IP:** generic phrase, no song or brand origin (KYM). Avoid celebrity examples and the political variants.

### 2. "Gold star behaviour" (TikTok + Instagram)
- **What:** Celebrating tiny adult wins as if earning a classroom gold star: "Gold star behaviour: remembering an umbrella".
- **Evidence:** Pepper, 1 Oct 2026. TikTok discover pages for "gold star sticker meme / praise" exist (undated), so the idea has a base. Single dated source.
- **Buyers:** planner and journaling crowd, ADHD/"adulting" community, teachers, coworker gift buyers.
- **Product:** print-then-cut sticker sheet of celestial gold stars with tiny wins ("drank water", "answered the email", "went outside"); mug/tee "Gold star behaviour" with a smiling star and moon; printable adult reward chart. Strong celestial fit.
- **Etsy competition (estimate):** "gold star sticker" is crowded (thousands, mostly kids' rewards), but "gold star behaviour" adult stickers probably under a few hundred.
- **Lifespan:** caption trend 3-6 weeks; adult-reward stickers are evergreen.
- **IP:** generic; check for a "GOLD STAR" apparel trademark before titling.

### 3. "Lived-in autumn" / "This fall I want more" small-moments content (TikTok)
- **What:** Unpolished autumn posts about small real moments ("putting the heating on for the first time", rainy commutes) and a carousel of small seasonal wishes.
- **Evidence:** Pepper, 1 Oct ("lived-in autumn"); Time Is Now, 2 Oct ("This fall I want more" carousel); SocialPilot hashtags week of 29 Sep (#cozyseason, #sweaterweather). Three sources in the same week, so it is the strongest-evidenced item here.
- **Buyers:** cozy-season planners, moms, journalers; people who already buy "fall bucket list" printables.
- **Product:** printable "small autumn moments" checklist/bucket list (heating on, first soup, leaf walk, candle season, first frost) in moody woodland art, plus a matching print-then-cut sticker sheet. Lists are the buyer's own, not a copied caption.
- **Etsy competition (estimate):** "fall bucket list printable" probably 3-8k listings; the "small moments" angle is less crowded. Under the 50k bar.
- **Lifespan:** through November; a winter version follows naturally.
- **IP:** none.

### 4. "Not to be dramatic, but..." hyper-specific seasonal observations (TikTok)
- **What:** Narrow, relatable statements, e.g. "Not to be dramatic, but this is the first acceptable week for knitwear".
- **Evidence:** Pepper, 1 Oct 2026. Single source.
- **Product:** mug "Not to be dramatic, but it's officially soup season" with a mushroom pot. Buyers: cozy-humour gift buyers.
- **Etsy competition (estimate):** "soup season" mugs are already thousands. Weak differentiation.
- **Lifespan:** 2-4 weeks. **Weak; not picked.**

### 5. "It was a bad dream" / everyday horror (TikTok, Halloween)
