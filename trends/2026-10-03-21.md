## Candidates

### 1. "Kinda chic to..." autumn homebody variant (TikTok + Instagram)
- **What:** Caption format "Kinda chic to [unglamorous thing]". It started as body-positivity/wellbeing (Apr-Aug 2026) and is now being reused for autumn: Pepper's example is "Kinda chic to cancel plans because it's raining".
- **Evidence:** Pepper, 1 Oct 2026 (listed as a current October format). Older context: KYM 14 Aug, Hola 18 Aug (searches "up 5,000%", celebrity posts). So it is a months-old format with a fresh seasonal turn, not a brand-new one. Moderate.
- **Buyers:** homebodies, introverts, readers, cozy-season fans buying for themselves or as a "this is so you" gift.
- **Product:** tee/mug/sticker in our own wording: "Kinda chic to stay in when it rains" (candle, teapot, sleeping fox), "Kinda chic to be a night owl" (owl on a crescent moon), "Kinda chic to forage instead of socialise" (mushrooms). One SVG set of 6 phrase+motif designs.
- **Etsy competition (estimate):** low hundreds for "kinda chic"; no listings surfaced in web search.
- **Lifespan:** format 4-8 more weeks; the homebody phrases sell through winter.
- **IP:** generic phrase, no song or brand origin (KYM). Avoid celebrity examples and the political variants.

### 2. "Gold star behaviour" (TikTok + Instagram)
- **What:** Celebrating tiny adult wins as if earning a classroom gold star: "Gold star behaviour: remembering an umbrella".
- **Evidence:** Pepper, 1 Oct 2026. TikTok discover pages for "gold star sticker meme / praise" exist (undated), so the idea has a base. Single dated source.
- **Buyers:** planner and journaling crowd, ADHD/"adulting" community, teachers, coworker gift buyers.
- **Product:** print-then-cut sticker sheet of celestial gold stars with tiny wins ("drank water", "answered the email", "went outside"); mug/tee "Gold star behaviour" with a smiling star and moon; printable adult reward chart. Strong celestial fit.
- **Etsy competition (estimate):** "gold star sticker" is crowded (thousands, mostly kids' rewards), but "gold star behaviour" adult stickers probably under a few hundred.
- **Lifespan:** caption trend 3-6 weeks; adult-reward stickers are evergreen.
- **IP:** generic; check for a "GOLD STAR" apparel trademark before titling.

### 3. "Lived-in autumn" / "This fall I want more" small-moments content (TikTok)

- **Buyers:** cozy-season planners, moms, journalers; people who already buy "fall bucket list" printables.

- **Etsy competition (estimate):** "fall bucket list printable" probably 3-8k listings; the "small moments" angle is less crowded. Under the 50k bar.
- **Lifespan:** through November; a winter version follows naturally.
- **IP:** none.

## Judge's verdicts (3 Oct 2026, scan 2)

Checks used: 11 searches/fetches. Pepper (1 Oct 2026) fetched and confirmed to exist with all three items. Etsy search can't be fetched (blocked), so listing counts are still unverified. Web searches for these exact phrases found no Etsy listings, which shows no buyer demand either way.

1. **"Kinda chic" homebody set: REJECTED.**
   - IP: passes. Searches for "kinda chic trademark" and a USPTO/2026 query found no KINDA CHIC mark, only unrelated CHIC-word marks (e.g. CHIC jeans reg. 1135189, CHIC STYLE, CHIC STAR). This is not a full TESS clearance.
   - Lifespan: fails. The format started in Apr 2026 and peaked in early Aug (Drew Barrymore post on 3 Aug, KYM, Hola 18 Aug). Pepper's 1 Oct mention is a recap, and I found no new October source. A months-old caption format will be stale by the time POD items arrive (17 Oct or later).
   - Demand: no evidence that anyone buys this. No "kinda chic to" products turned up on Etsy or anywhere else. That leaves views only, which fails the buyer-intent test.
   - Taste: the original variants carry body, sobriety and political themes ("pro-choice tradwife"). Our wording avoids them, but the caption's identity is tied to them.
   - Fallback: the homebody/night-owl lines can go in an evergreen cozy set without "kinda chic". Not queued as a trend.
2. **"Lived-in autumn" small-moments printable + sticker sheet: APPROVED (narrowly).**
   - IP: none. It is a content mood, not a phrase, and the list wording is ours.
   - Demand: real buyer intent exists for fall bucket list / autumn checklist printables. They sell on Etsy (e.g. BitterLemonDigital listing 836388865, ~CA$4.41), on Payhip ($1.50-$3.99) and on Gumroad ($2.99). It is a crowded field with many free versions, and the Hunter's 3-8k is probably low.
   - Angle: "small real moments" (heating on, first soup, first frost) instead of generic pumpkin-patch lists, in moody woodland art with a matching sticker sheet. That is a clear differentiation.
   - Evidence: three dated sources in one week (Pepper 1 Oct; Time Is Now 2 Oct; SocialPilot hashtags, week of 29 Sep).
   - Lifespan: it is a digital download, so there is no POD delay, and it sells through November.
   - Makeable: code-drawn type and line art cost $0.
   - Caveat: generic fall bucket lists peak in September, so list it this week or not at all.
3. **"Gold star behaviour" sticker sheet + mug: REJECTED.**
   - Lifespan: fails. Pepper itself calls it "September's 'Gold Star Behaviour' trend", and other roundups place it in Aug-Sep 2026. It will be ~2 months old by the time POD items arrive.
   - Demand: "gold star sticker" is crowded with kids' reward stickers, and adult-wins stickers are evergreen, not a trend.
   - Trademark check on "GOLD STAR":
     - GOLD STAR FC DETROIT (sn 97802713): new application, covers stickers, decals and t-shirts/hoodies.
     - GOLDSTAR (sn 97454941): class 25, t-shirts/knit shirts.
     - Gold-star-with-runner design mark: registered for tees and hoodies.
     - STAR GOLDEN GOOSE (sn 79416095): registered, clothing.
     - Plain GOLD STAR apparel marks 78440599 (abandoned 2005) and 0236936 (expired).
     - No GOLD STAR BEHAVIO(U)R mark found.
     - The phrase is not cleanly free for apparel or stickers.
   - Taste: "Gold Star" is also the US term for families of fallen service members, so "Gold Star ..." apparel titles risk causing offence.
